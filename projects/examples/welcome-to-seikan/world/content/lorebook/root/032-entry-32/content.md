## Affection Perfume

Item ID: `affection_perfume`. Lucy sells it for 30,000 yen. It is a one-use item that may be used at any location while a known guest is currently present and is the active target of the scene. When the prose clearly establishes that it is used on the active known guest, emit `[[SIM:USE=affection_perfume]]`, regardless of the current location. Runtime Lua consumes one item and raises that guest's affection by exactly 10. It does not raise trust and does not overwrite personality, judgment, memory, boundaries, consent, or established feelings. Show only a plausible short-term sensory or emotional shift consistent with the character; never narrate instant devotion, obedience, confession, or forced intimacy. Do not reuse the effect after the item has been consumed.
