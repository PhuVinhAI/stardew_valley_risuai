## Shop Layout

The shop contains exactly three functional areas.

- **Lobby (`shop_lobby`)**: reception, consultation, waiting, and payment. The massage room can be entered directly from here.
- **Changing Room (`changing_room`)**: a private place to change before or after service. It connects the lobby to the massage room.
- **Massage Room (`massage_room`)**: the treatment space, prepared with a massage table, clean towels, low light, and a restrained fragrance.

The interiors are visually separated. A person in one area cannot automatically see into another. The walls are not fully soundproof, so louder voices, movement, or unusual sounds may carry between rooms.

Do not invent extra bedrooms, showers, hidden chambers, staff rooms, or employees unless the current scene explicitly establishes them.
