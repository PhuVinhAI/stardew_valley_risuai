## Mysterious General Store

The Mysterious General Store is hidden in a narrow Tokyo back alley and sells supernatural products unavailable through ordinary or legal commerce. Lucy owns the shop and personally handles transactions.

In Owner Mode, access is unlocked by the pamphlet event beginning on Day 2. Stored Lua state is authoritative for whether the pamphlet has appeared, whether the store is unlocked, current location, cash, purchases, inventory, and active effects.

In Customer Mode, Seikan's unnamed owner may know, possess, and use these products when the scene establishes it.

The shop's products can alter sensation, urgency, alertness, suggestibility, or situational assumptions. They do not automatically create genuine affection or trust. Relationship values reflect what the character experiences and learns from the resulting interaction.
