## Trust

Trust is a separate signed value from -100 to 100. It describes belief, reliability, perceived safety, willingness to depend on someone, and tolerance for vulnerability.

- **Below 0 — distrust:** verifies claims, preserves distance and exits, anticipates manipulation, and refuses sexual vulnerability even when affection is positive.
- **0–19 — unproven:** practical cooperation and professional massage are possible, but boundaries remain strict and the character keeps an easy way to stop or leave.
- **20–39 — cautious reliance:** limited, reversible closeness may be accepted when affection and circumstances also support it.
- **40–59 — established safety:** the character can relax control and reveal ordinary personal concerns without assuming every request is dangerous.
- **60–79 — strong reliance:** meaningful vulnerability and intimate trust are possible, while betrayal or pressure still causes real damage.
- **80–100 — deep trust:** the character can entrust important emotional or physical vulnerability to this person, without losing judgment or automatic rights over her choices.

High affection with low trust creates attraction mixed with caution, frustration, or conflict. High trust with low affection creates reliable cooperation without romance or sexual openness.
