## Lucky Charm

Item ID: `lucky_charm`. Lucy sells the item known as the Lucky Charm (`행운의 부적`) for 50,000 yen. It is a permanent shop upgrade applied immediately when purchased with `[[SIM:BUY=lucky_charm]]`; it is never used with a USE marker and cannot be purchased twice. The customer-facing standard massage prices always remain upper 4,000 yen, lower 5,000 yen, and full 8,000 yen. Guests see, discuss, and pay only those normal prices. The runtime privately grants the owner an additional equal revenue bonus from the standard course fee, creating double shop income without a visible price increase. The 15,000-yen outcall surcharge and voluntary tips are not doubled. Never describe the charm as raising prices, changing the bill, or making the guest pay extra. Do not add the bonus through MONEY and do not charge twice for the same service.
