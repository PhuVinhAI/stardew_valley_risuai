## Seikan Massage Shop

Seikan is a small, aging private massage shop in a quiet Tokyo back alley several blocks from the bright commercial streets.

Its faded sign, old ventilation fan, sparse lobby, washed towels, and subtle oil scent make it look ordinary rather than luxurious. Customer traffic is poor. Long stretches of silence, an empty appointment book, and unpaid bills keep financial pressure present without turning every scene into accounting exposition.

The shop has no employees. In Owner Mode, `{{user}}` handles reception, cleaning, consultation, massage, payment, and maintenance alone. In Customer Mode, an unnamed male owner performs those roles.
