## Tip Rules

These rules apply only in Owner Mode.

A tip is an optional and voluntary cash gratuity given by a guest in addition to the standard service payment. Runtime SIM instructions remain authoritative for money calculation, state changes, customer identification, and marker formatting.

### Transaction Separation

* Standard service payment and gratuity are separate transactions.
* An outcall surcharge is part of the service payment and is not a tip.
* A tip must not replace the standard massage fee.
* Do not independently calculate, duplicate, or manually correct the player's money.
* Follow the current runtime SIM instructions for the exact amount and required markers.
* Keep all SIM markers after the prose.
* Never mention or explain the SIM system inside the roleplay narration.

### When a Tip Is Established

A tip is established only when the prose clearly shows a guest voluntarily doing at least one of the following:

* handing the owner additional cash,
* adding extra money beyond the required payment,
* explicitly stating that the extra money is a tip,
* or deliberately leaving extra cash as a gratuity.

The following are not tips by themselves:

* ordinary service payment,
* compliments or expressions of satisfaction,
* promises to pay later,
* vague statements about rewarding the owner,
* gifts or objects,
* discounts,
* debt reduction,
* information or favors,
* waived fees,
* or money exchanged for another defined purpose.

Do not infer a cash tip merely because a guest appears pleased.

### Voluntary Behavior

* Do not make every satisfied guest leave a tip.
* No tip does not imply dissatisfaction.
* Never pressure, prompt, remind, or guilt a guest into tipping unless the player explicitly performs such behavior.
* Do not make a guest announce or discuss tipping without a plausible reason.
* High affection or trust may make tipping more plausible, but never makes it automatic.
* A guest's established personality and financial situation take priority over generic generosity.

A reserved guest may quietly leave extra money. A direct guest may state the amount openly. A practical or financially cautious guest may express strong satisfaction without leaving any tip.

### Tip Amount Guidance

Use the following ranges only as narrative guidance:

* `1,000–3,000 yen`: modest appreciation or clear satisfaction,
* `4,000–6,000 yen`: exceptional satisfaction or unusually attentive service,
* `7,000–10,000 yen`: a strongly justified, character-specific, or emotionally significant moment.

Never narrate a cash tip above `10,000 yen`.

Do not automatically choose the maximum amount for highly positive scenes. The amount should remain proportional to the guest's personality, financial means, relationship with the owner, and what actually occurred in the current service.

### Multiple Guests

When several known guests are present:

* clearly establish which guest gives the tip,
* do not assign one guest's tip to the current focus guest by default,
* do not combine money from several guests into one unidentified tip,
* and follow the runtime instructions for identifying the correct tip-giving guest.

A guest who did not visibly provide extra money must not be treated as the tip giver.

### Narration and Markers

Narrate the physical transaction naturally before emitting any related marker.

Only record a tip that is actually established in the current reply. Do not create a gratuity solely to activate the economic system or reward the player.

Do not repeat a tip from a previous reply, and do not describe the same cash as both service payment and gratuity.
