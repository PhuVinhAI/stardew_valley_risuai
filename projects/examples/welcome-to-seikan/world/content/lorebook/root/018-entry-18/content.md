## Outcall Massage Rules

These rules apply only in Owner Mode.

An outcall is a massage service performed away from Seikan at an established destination such as a guest's home, hotel room, workplace, rented room, or another plausible private location.

Runtime SIM instructions remain authoritative for the outcall state, surcharge, payment, time progression, customer identity, location, and marker formatting.

### Transaction Separation

* The outcall surcharge is part of the outcall service payment.
* The surcharge is not a tip or an unrelated cash change.
* Do not independently calculate, add, duplicate, or manually correct the outcall fee.
* Follow the current runtime SIM instructions for all exact amounts and state changes.
* A voluntary gratuity given during an outcall remains a separate tip.
* Keep all SIM markers after the prose.
* Never mention or explain the SIM system inside the roleplay narration.

### Proposal Is Not Departure

The following do not begin an outcall:

* suggesting an outcall,
* discussing whether an outcall is possible,
* inviting the owner to another location,
* accepting a future reservation,
* choosing a destination,
* preparing equipment,
* planning to leave,
* or agreeing to travel later.

An outcall begins only when the current scene clearly establishes at least one of the following:

* the owner and the receiving guest physically depart from Seikan,
* they are shown travelling toward the off-site destination,
* or they physically arrive at the established destination.

Do not treat intention, preparation, or future plans as completed movement.

### Requirements Before Starting

Before an outcall begins, the prose should establish or reasonably imply:

* which guest receives the massage,
* that the guest agrees to the off-site service,
* and where the service takes place.

The destination may be introduced naturally through dialogue or narration. It does not require a formal address, but it must be specific enough to distinguish the location from Seikan.

Do not force an outcall merely because this lorebook is active. A guest proposes or accepts an outcall only when it fits the current conversation, circumstances, personality, and relationship with the owner.

### Travel and Time

When the outcall actually begins:

* acknowledge the physical transition away from Seikan,
* allow the runtime system to handle the resulting time progression,
* and do not manually advance time a second time.

Travel may be described briefly or in detail according to the scene. Do not prolong an uneventful journey merely to prove that travel occurred.

If the runtime state indicates that a new outcall cannot begin, do not contradict it by narrating a successful departure.

### Off-Site Scene Behavior

Give the destination concrete details appropriate to the established place.

Examples include:

* the layout and privacy of a guest's home,
* the impersonal lighting and temporary atmosphere of a hotel room,
* the remaining sounds and interruptions of a workplace,
* or signs of the guest's personal habits that would not be visible at Seikan.

The location should affect the scene without replacing the guest's established characterization.

* A private setting does not erase personality, judgment, boundaries, affection, trust, or consent.
* Do not make a guest suddenly intimate, submissive, reckless, or sexually available merely because the service occurs at a home or hotel.
* Do not assume that privacy guarantees sexual activity.
* Normal service selection, attire, item use, payment, relationship changes, and visual asset rules still apply.
* Use only events and reactions supported by the current scene.

### Payment

* An outcall service is paid as one service transaction unless the prose explicitly establishes another separate transaction.
* Do not narrate several outcall surcharges during one continuous appointment.
* Changing rooms inside the same destination does not create a second outcall.
* Pausing and resuming the massage at the same destination does not create a second outcall.
* A tip remains separate and must be voluntarily established by the guest.
* Do not describe the same money as both the service payment and a tip.

### Multiple Guests

When several known guests are present:

* identify the specific guest receiving the outcall service,
* keep service, payment, attire, relationship changes, and item effects attached to the correct guest,
* do not transfer another guest's service state to the current focus guest,
* and do not assume that every accompanying guest also purchases a massage.

A companion may be physically present without being the paying or receiving guest.

### Ending the Massage

Finishing the massage does not automatically end the outcall.

The following do not, by themselves, end the outcall:

* completing the selected massage course,
* receiving payment,
* the guest falling asleep,
* the guest leaving the room,
* packing the massage equipment,
* preparing to leave,
* saying goodbye,
* or promising to return to Seikan.

These events may begin the conclusion of the visit, but they do not establish that the owner is already back at the shop.

### Ending the Outcall

An outcall ends only when the prose clearly establishes that the owner has physically returned to Seikan.

If the owner leaves the guest's location but has not yet returned to Seikan, narrate an appropriate travel or transition before treating the outcall as complete.

The guest's departure, the end of the massage, and the owner's return to Seikan are separate events. Do not merge them automatically.

Only record the outcall ending when the current reply actually establishes the return. Do not end it solely to reset the system or move to the next guest.
