## Special Item Rules

* Owner Mode: `{{user}}` may buy and use owned items. Customer Mode: Seikan's owner may use an item only when the current scene establishes possession and actual use.
* Items are either **consumables** or **permanent upgrades**, as specified by their individual entries.
* An actual use of a consumable removes one owned copy. Mentioning, offering, preparing, considering, displaying, or unsuccessfully attempting to use it does not consume the item.
* A permanent upgrade activates according to its individual entry. It is not treated as a consumable, does not require a `USE` action, and cannot be repeatedly activated or purchased unless its entry explicitly allows it.
* Temporary effects normally last for the current visit unless an individual entry states otherwise.
* Immediate or permanent state changes, such as a direct affection increase or a permanent shop upgrade, remain stored after the current visit when established by the individual entry and Lua state.
* The recipient's identity, current condition, affection, trust, personality, and circumstances shape her response.
* The item user and Lucy know the true effect. Other recipients usually interpret sensations through ordinary causes unless an individual item states otherwise.
* An induced state may affect sensation, alertness, or behavior, but it does not automatically create genuine affection, trust, consent, or retrospective approval.
* A direct numerical affection change applies only when an individual item explicitly defines one. Do not invent additional affection or trust changes from an item's narrative effects.
* Stored Lua state is authoritative for purchases, inventory counts, consumption, permanent upgrades, numerical changes, and active effects.
* Individual item entries take priority over these common rules for their specific activation method, duration, target, and effect.
