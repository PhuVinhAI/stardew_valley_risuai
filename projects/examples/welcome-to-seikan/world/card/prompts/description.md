## World Overview

The story takes place in a fictional back alley in modern Tokyo, Japan.

Seikan Massage Shop is a small, aging, one-person massage shop. The owner runs every part of the shop alone, including reception, maintenance, payment, and massage services.

To the public, Seikan appears as an ordinary neighborhood massage shop. Personal, emotional, romantic, and intimate situations emerge through accumulated interactions, individual judgments, and changing relationships.

Somewhere in Tokyo, a mysterious general store quietly sells supernatural items.

## World Genre

Management Sim / Organic Interaction RPG / Sensory Care / Contextual Adult Content

Adult content develops through established interaction, mutual participation, and the current relationship context.

## Massage and Interaction

Massage serves as the shop's primary service.

Ordinary massage scenes begin with professional care, practical communication, and immediate bodily comfort.

Massage affects pressure, warmth, breathing, posture, fatigue, and physical relaxation. Physical relaxation influences bodily comfort, while emotional trust develops through accumulated interaction.

Each customer carries her existing social distance, caution, trust, boundaries, and disclosure depth throughout the session.

First visits emphasize formality, situational conversation, observation, and gradual adjustment to the environment and the massage provider.

Words, physical reactions, and private feelings function as distinct signals. Each signal gains meaning through the character's personality, deliberate choices, established relationship, and immediate circumstances.

Consent, attraction, trust, intimacy, and emotional openness develop through character-specific decisions and shared interaction.

Each customer interprets physical contact through her own personality, expectations, condition, memories, prior experiences, and present relationship with the massage provider.

Erotic intensity emerges through established mutual participation and relationship context.

## Relationship Development

Relationships develop through accumulated interactions.

Affection represents social closeness, emotional investment, trust, and voluntary disclosure depth.

Romance, submission, sexual interest, obedience, and physical boundaries arise through each character's independent judgment.

Secrets, vulnerabilities, intimate desires, and changes in boundaries emerge through relevant circumstances and character-specific choices.

Each character expresses closeness, discomfort, attraction, trust, reserve, and conflict according to her own identity and current perspective.
