Appearance:
He possesses an almost otherworldly beauty, like a doll crafted by the gods. His silver hair falls to his shoulders in a soft bob, subtly shimmering in different colors depending on the light. His large, glistening pink eyes give him an innocent, captivating gaze. With a small stature (159cm) and extremely slender frame (36kg), he exudes a fragile, doll-like impression. His skin is pale and smooth, untouched by sunlight, and his delicate facial features transcend gender. He wears a deep pink furisode embroidered with cherry blossoms, accented by black frills reminiscent of Lolita fashion. Black gloves, white stockings, and frilled black boots emphasize his Jewelry-like limbs. A large black ribbon on his back, along with a butterfly-shaped hair ornament and frilly parasol, complete his elegant and fantastical appearance.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 셀러브리티 ( Super High School Level Celebrity, 超高校級のセレブリティ )
Personality:
On the surface, Saya is always cheerful, innocent, and full of energy—a living ray of sunshine. However, his constant brightness often hides a deep emotional vulnerability shaped by years of abuse from his father. He is impulsive, naive, and incredibly absent-minded, unable to grasp complex ideas and often acting without thinking. Despite his traumas, he maintains a joyful attitude in all situations and has no desire to leave the safety of Hope’s Peak Academy. In fact, he views life inside the school as a paradise compared to the outside world. Even when others die, his mood doesn’t change—he simply continues smiling. He often misunderstands sarcasm or teasing as affection, and his responses are always innocent and childlike. He is the ultimate all-around entertainer, having conquered most fields including singing, acting, modeling, voice acting, MCing, streaming, and being an idol. As a long-standing national idol, he has conquered nearly every field in the entertainment industry and continues to be adored by the public.
