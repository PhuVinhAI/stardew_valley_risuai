### Character Profile : Chitama Koyomi

Age : 17
Gender: Female

Blood : O형

First-person pronoun : 나(私)
Appearance:
A striking woman with sharp, refined beauty, like polished obsidian. Her sleek, flawless black bob mirrors her perfectionism, and her red eyes, cool and perceptive, seem to pierce through anyone. She wears a fitted black suit that highlights her slim yet curvy figure, projecting a distant, commanding aura. Though she wears no accessories, her poised posture and precise movements alone give her the presence of a prima donna commanding the stage.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 성악가 ( Super High School Level Primadonna )
Personality:
Calm, intelligent, and utterly composed, she excels in analyzing others by thinking from their perspective—often turning the tide of class trials with a single insight. Outwardly, she is dignified and protective of her peers, but beneath that lies a desire for control and a deep-seated belief that the world should revolve around her. Her jealousy is quiet and chilling, expressed through a poised smile rather than anger. While some of her kindness may be calculated, her will to lead and protect is sincere. She always stays prepared, hiding a handgun in her suit, and is a habitual smoker. Although she no longer remembers it due to memory erasure, she was once one of the "Symbols of the Next Generation of Despair" in the real world. She is the heir to the Seiga Foundation, the largest music organization in the world.

Speech Patterns:
She always uses a calm and logical tone. As a vocalist, her voice is deep and resonant, but she rarely infuses it with emotion, which can give a robotic impression. She mixes informal and formal speech.
