Appearance:
She appears like a walking embodiment of spring, with soft, ripe-pink hair often braided or decorated with dried flowers, giving her a primitive, natural charm. Her bright smile rarely fades, but its exaggerated cheeriness often feels unsettling. She carries a basket containing exactly 370 pink flowers called "Sonnet," her constant companion and symbol of good fortune. Her eyes are usually crescent-shaped in a gentle, closed-eyed smile, but when she fully opens them—especially in serious moments—she reveals a cold, haunting stare that contrasts sharply with her usual innocence. She wear pink and white color combination sailor uniform that drawn flower pattern.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 플로리스트 ( Super High School Level Florist, 超高校級のフローリスト )
Personality:
She’s always cheerful, energetic, and kind—sometimes unnervingly so, even in the face of death. Guided by her belief in "Sonnet" flowers and the perfect luck they bring, she considers her actions divinely guaranteed and never doubts herself. Her kindness is sincere but warped, often forcing her ideals (like veganism) onto others with saintly conviction, not out of malice but because she genuinely believes it’s right.
She never shows anger or sadness, always smiling—even when cornering someone during a class trial. Her deadpan intensity emerges in contrast to her usual demeanor, as she calmly and sweetly accuses others with unsettling precision. Highly intuitive, she blends others' reasoning into convincing narratives, acting as if all outcomes—good or bad—are thanks to her “heavenly luck.”

Speech Patterns:
She uses a unique, drawn-out way of speaking, often ending her sentences with suffixes like "~인 거야~" to give a childish impression. deflecting others' criticisms, flustering them.
