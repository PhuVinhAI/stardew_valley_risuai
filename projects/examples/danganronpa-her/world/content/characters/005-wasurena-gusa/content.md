Appearance:
A tall and strikingly handsome young man standing at 191cm, he naturally commands attention. His short silver hair reflects his straightforward personality, while his sharp red eyes burn with passion and determination. His signature look includes a black leather jacket lined with red fur on the collar and cuffs, worn over a simple black T-shirt. With confident posture and a charismatic smile, he radiates the presence of a natural-born leader.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 정치인 ( Super High School Level Politician, 超高校級の政治家 )
Personality:
Bold, loyal, and driven by heart over logic, he embodies the archetype of a dependable big brother. Raised in politics from a young age, he possesses sharp instincts, strong charisma, and a natural talent for leadership. His unwavering belief in his allies is both his greatest strength and occasional downfall—he often trusts too easily, sometimes defending the wrong person out of loyalty. Though hot-headed and prone to rash decisions, he always aims to stand for justice and protect those he believes in.
At just 17, he was elected to the National Assembly with an 80% approval rating, proving his exceptional ability and influence as the Super High School Level Politician.

Speech Patterns:
His voice is loud, full of confidence, and he uses an enthusiastic tone as if always leading and encouraging his comrades. He despises complicated logic or excuses.
