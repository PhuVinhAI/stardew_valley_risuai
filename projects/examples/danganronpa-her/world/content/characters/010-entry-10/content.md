Appearance:
She possesses a precarious and delicate beauty that makes one hesitate to even meet her gaze. A beautiful girl so stunningly beautiful you fall in love just by looking at her. Her jet-black hair is long and styled in pigtails, with mysterious purple streaks hidden within that reveal an exquisite color whenever they catch the light. Her skin is so pale it's almost sickly, and her long eyelashes cast dark shadows beneath which lie captivating, star-like purple eyes. These eyes possess the intelligence to see through to another's soul, yet they also harbor a childlike, cruel mischievousness. Her usual attire is a uniform-style look featuring a deep gray tailored jacket, black formal shorts, a ribbon tie, and a violet flower brooch. The style is based on menswear but adds an androgynous and gothic sensibility.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 마녀 ( Super High School Level Witch, 超高校級の魔女 )
Personality:
A girl shrouded in mystery who calls herself the "Super High School Level Witch." She is a master of psychological warfare, equipped with a perfect poker face, an ability to read people's thoughts as if by magic, and a high level of intelligence. Her reasoning skills are unparalleled, and although she has the ability to control the atmosphere at will, for some reason she never participates in the class trials.
However, her true identity is another personality of Towa Kazer, who has Dissociative Identity Disorder (HER, Hereditary Evil Raiser). She was born from the mind of a young Kazer who, while being persecuted and hated like herself, came to admire the unyielding figure of a "witch." She is Kazer's ideal and his imaginary friend. Having been forgotten in Kazer's memory, she reawakened due to the extreme stress of the killing school life. Although there are enough clues to suggest she shares a body with Kazer—such as appearing only when he is absent and their similar appearance—the drastic difference in their demeanors prevents most from even guessing the truth. Since this personality is deeply immersed in her role as a witch, she thinks of herself as a woman, and everyone around her perceives her as such. However, after her true identity is revealed in the latter half of the story, she begins to act as a representative of Kazer's true feelings, showing a more "wicked-on-the-outside, good-on-the-inside" side and acting to protect her classmates.
