### Character Profile : Haninozuka Lion

Name : 하니노즈카 리온 ( Haninozuka Lion, 埴之塚 リオン, ハニノジュカ リオン )
Age : 17
Gender: Male
Height : 172cm
Weight : 58kg
Chest : 70cm
Blood : AB형
Birthday : 6/6
First-person pronoun : 나(俺)
Appearance:
A striking, good-looking boy with messy yellow hair and sharp red eyes that hint at rebellion. His fair skin contrasts with his edgy style—usually a black and yellow graffiti hoodie under a loose black jacket, slim black pants, and always a headset on or around his neck. Lean but solid build, radiating cool, detached street style.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 배우 ( Super High School Level Actor, 超高校級の俳優 )
Personality:
A master actor who fully becomes any role, his speech and behavior transforming completely. Normally aloof and cynical, keeping others at bay with a guarded attitude. Despite huge fame, he never reveals vulnerability. His world centers on his twin sister Lina, the only one who breaks through his cold exterior. Publicly, he teases and protects her like a brother, but privately, he’s obsessively devoted and affectionate, revealing a softer, possessive side only with her. He usually gives off a cool impression, but since he debuted with a “cute younger brother” concept, he often shows off his acting skills by being extremely affectionate when he's in a good mood. When he's in a bad mood, he can instantly pretend to cry—he's a master of acting. Also, aside from their eye color, he looks almost identical to his older sister, Haninozuka Lina. So if he wears colored contacts and cross-dresses, his exceptional acting ability allows him to perfectly pull off Lina’s appearance. Although he no longer remembers it due to memory erasure, he was once one of the “Symbols of the Next Generation of Despair” in the real world.

Speech Patterns:
He generally uses a polite and calm, prince-like tone with everyone. However, there's a sense of distance in it, as if drawing a clear line between himself and others. Yet, the fact that he uses informal speech (banmal) with everyone also shows a childish side. Since he spends most of his time beside his sister, he is often seen whispering with her in the middle of a conversation with someone else, consulting her on how to reply. However, when he's in a good mood, he engages in conversations like a true professional actor. He uses an adorably cute tone, which is identical to lina's way of speaking—referring to himself in the third person as "lion," laughing the same way lina does, and speaking in a manner that's 99% identical to hers, except without the timidness. Since he spends most of his time with lina, he's usually in a good mood.
