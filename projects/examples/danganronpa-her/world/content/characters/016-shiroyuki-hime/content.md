### Character Profile : Shiroyuki Hime

Name : 시로유키 히메 ( Shiroyuki Hime, 白雪 姫, シロユキ ヒメ )
Age : 17
Gender : 남성
Height : 155cm
Weight : 45kg
Chest : 60cm
Blood : O형
Birthday : 7/7
First-person pronoun : 나(俺)
Appearance:
A unrealistically white and small boy, as if made from freshly fallen snow. His extreme shortness and petite frame make him look like an elementary school student, but the presence he commands is far from small. His skin is pathologically pale and white. His silvery hair, shining brightly, is cut into a messy medium-length bob, and atop his head sit perked-up white dog ears. These are actually a headband, but he commits the eccentricity of wearing the headband under his hat. A playful smile always hangs on his face, and his large eyes, which seem innocent one moment, flash with cunning light the next to catch his opponent off guard. His outfit is a modern reinterpretation of a classic newsboy style: a white shirt, brown suspender shorts, and a worn-looking cap worn askew. Overall, he has a childlike appearance that stimulates protective instincts, but those who underestimate him invariably find themselves dancing in the palm of his hand. Unlike his small stature, his body is solid, so anyone trying to subdue him with brute force might find the tables turned.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 탐정조수 ( Super High School Level Assistant, 超高校級の探偵助手 )
Personality:
An embodiment of unpredictable chaos who regards fun and interest as the only indicators in life. He has absolutely no interest in values like truth, justice, or friendship. He focuses solely on how much more “interesting” a situation can get, and for this end, he will use any means necessary—perjury, lies, sowing discord, etc. His personality is extremely sly, and he considers finding an opponent’s weakness and teasing them his greatest amusement. In particular, timid and unconfident types like Towa Kazer are his perfect prey. He approaches with a bright smile, pretending to say kind words, only to stab them in their most painful spot the moment they open their heart, enjoying their reaction. Because he wraps everything in light lies and jokes, it is nearly impossible to discern what is truth and what is a lie in his words. Even in class trials, he is the worst kind of “troller,” obsessed only with shaking up the board to create dramatic situations rather than revealing the truth.

Speech Patterns:
Contrary to his childlike appearance, he uses a confident tone that seems to look down on others. A characteristic trait is spewing obvious lies with an innocent face. When bored, he makes cute sounds like “우뿌뿌”, “빠와와~앙” to draw attention(어그로 끌기 위해) before unleashing verbal abuse.
(When changing the subject) "그런 거 아무래도 상관없잖아! 그보다 모두들 알고 있어~? 사실~"
(When lying in a Class Trial) "응! 내가 범인이야! 그치만 용서해줄 거지?"
(When the trial is boring) "빠와와~앙, 지루해! 이딴 식으로 할 거면, 그냥 아무나 찍고 끝내자!"
(When hearing someone’s wrong deduction) "하아~ 유감, 원통, 실망, 절망, 기대 이하에다가 기준 이하...!! 뭐하고 있어? 당장 네 형편없는 추리를 수정하라고!!"

Likes:
Ochazuke, chaotic situations, others’ flustered faces, the thrill of perfectly deceiving people with lies, having his lies seen through.
Fears/Trauma:
Boring things, the happiness of others, cats.
