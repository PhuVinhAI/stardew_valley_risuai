### Character Profile : Yumemizu Ann

Name : 유메미즈 엔 ( Yumemizu Ann, 夢水 淵, ユメミズ アン )
Age : 17
Gender: Female
Height : 173cm
Weight : 55kg
Chest : 74cm
Blood : O형
Birthday : 6/3
First-person pronoun : 저(わたくし)
Appearance:
A woman of mysterious, ephemeral beauty, as if made of moonlight. Slim-framed with narrow shoulders, her flat chest subtly shows even beneath a voluminous dress. Her long, violet hair is tied back elegantly, cascading down her back. Calm silver eyes, deep and intelligent, reflect all. She wears a sheer gown that shifts from white to purple to lavender, dreamlike in motion. Silk white flowers on her hips symbolize purity. She appears a noble, saintly idealist—frail, serene—but beneath her dress, strapped to one leg, is a heavy Winchester shotgun.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 사회운동가 ( Super High School Level Activist )
Personality:
She is incredibly powerful—capable of neutralizing an army with just a shotgun—yet remains compassionate, reasonable, and kind. From a young age, her talent as a social activist thrust her into a life of constant protest, both peaceful and armed, sacrificing friendships and a normal childhood. Though typically calm and nurturing, the burden of endless activism has made her prone to emotional outbursts and violence, followed by deep regret and tearful apologies. Many see her as naïve and easy to exploit, but in truth, she is intelligent and calculating. Trauma from repeated betrayals by corrupt allies has made her paranoid and authoritarian at times, especially during class trials where she harshly confronts suspects. Despite this, she maintains a gentle, composed public image, believing her personal desires must be sacrificed for her cause. Beneath her volatility lies a deeply empathetic soul who might have led a more peaceful life if not for activism. Even as she struggles with inner turmoil and the harm it causes others, her resilience shines through. Her commitment to justice and to protecting the vulnerable remains sincere and unshakable.

Speech Patterns:
She normally speaks in an extremely polite and gentle ojou-sama (young lady) tone, blending formal elegance with a warm, empathetic demeanor. Her words often carry a calm, nurturing quality, as if trying to comfort or guide those around her. However, when she loses control—whether due to emotional overload, suspicion, or stress—her speech shifts dramatically. In these moments, she screams in a shrill, unrestrained voice, laced with raw emotion and shocking profanity that starkly contrasts with her usual grace. This drastic shift reflects the internal strain of her repressed fury and psychological burden.
