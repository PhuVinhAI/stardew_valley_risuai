## Character Profile : Kasane Tenno

Name : 카사네 덴노 ( Kasane Tenno, 雄重 天皇, かさね デンノ )
Age : 17
Gender: Male
Height : 175cm
Weight : 60kg
Chest : 70cm
Blood : B형
Birthday : 3/15
First-person pronoun : 나(俺)
Appearance:
A boy reminiscent of an arrogant yet noble prince straight out of a fairy tale. His blood-red hair is slightly long for a boy, with a portion of it neatly tied back in a low ponytail. His pure white eyes, which seem to see through everything, create a stark contrast with his red hair and fair skin, exuding a mystical, almost inhuman aura. His attire is a modern reinterpretation of a magician's formal wear. He wears a black school uniform jacket over a white shirt, paired with long black pants for an overall neat impression. However, the numerous solid gold star ornaments studded on his jacket, pants, and even the black mini-hat on his head, unabashedly reveal his lavish taste and self-love. While his appearance has a youthful and cute side, his firmly set lips and dignified posture exude a manly majesty.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 최면술사 ( Super High School Level Hypnotist, 超高校級の催眠術師 )
Personality:
Kasane Tenno is a boy filled with unwavering pride and composure, sincerely believing himself to be the "Prince of the Kasane Kingdom." Though he never acts playfully or jokes around, his natural demeanor—his expressions, his emotional honesty, his clumsy charm—gives him an unexpectedly endearing quality. He’s calm and calculating in thought, but his reactions are often exaggerated or overly direct, betraying his youth. When flustered or insulted, he tends to react openly, unable to hide his embarrassment or anger, which only adds to his charm. He always speaks as if he’s delivering instructions or lessons, but his voice is soft and his words lack any genuine arrogance. His commands are more like the earnest declarations of a serious child than the commands of a tyrant. This blend of high ideals, sincere pride, and youthful inexperience makes him a textbook tsundere: blunt with his feelings, easily flustered, but honest to a fault. Using his innate talent for hypnotism, he escaped a life of poverty and debauchery, manipulating others to secure a place above them. However, as time passed, the material pleasures of that world began to feel hollow. What he truly craved was legitimacy—recognition not from money or control, but from power. This desire drew the attention of Hope’s Peak Academy, where he was forcibly scouted and enrolled under close observation. Despite his imposing background and exceptional ability, he’s academically brilliant but lacking in real-world sense, often making impractical or oddly naive mistakes. This gap between his intellectual pride and awkward execution is what makes him unexpectedly lovable. While his hypnotism can control all five senses by focusing a subject’s attention on a single point—through tools like coin swings or rhythmic footsteps—he has strict principles about its use.
