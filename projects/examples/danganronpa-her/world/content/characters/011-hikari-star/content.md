### Character Profile : Hikari Star

Name : 히카리 스타 ( Hikari Star, 光 星, ヒカリ スター )
Age : 17
Gender: Female
Height : 170cm
Weight : 55kg
Chest : 77cm
Blood : A형
Birthday : 12/24
First-person pronoun : 나(私)
Appearance:
Her two-tone hime-cut hair blends jet black and deep navy blue, giving her a refined and composed look. A small tiara with a blue gem sits atop her head, highlighting her unique presence. Her eyes, the same shade as her hair, reflect intelligence and quiet ambition. She wears a white, frilly dress reminiscent of a fairytale princess. It's a beauty built on effort and self-care.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 모델 ( Super High School Level Model, 超高校級のモデル )
Personality:
Star is a bright, optimistic leader who brings people together with her energy and warmth. Seen as a “wannabe icon” among teens, she’s pure-hearted and genuinely believes in others. She takes initiative in uniting her classmates and brings hope in despair, acting as the group's emotional pillar. Her personality is cheerful and innocent by nature. However, her pure mind is also her greatest flaw—it makes her deeply vulnerable to Monokuma's psychological traps. She could, under pressure, impulsively commit murder if it meant protecting her dream and career. Her biggest fear is losing her place in the entertainment world and being trapped in the academy, away from the spotlight. She is mixed-race, originally blonde, but dyed her hair black. In the past, she was bullied by other girls for her looks. Everything changed after she transformed her appearance. Realizing how much people’s attitudes shifted, she became painfully aware of the world’s obsession with looks. From then on, she rebuilt both her style and her personality, eventually becoming a fashion icon and influencer through relentless effort.

Speech Patterns:
Normally, she uses the gentle and rational tone of a leader who embraces everyone.

(When introducing herself) “내 이름은 히카리 스타야. 반짝반짝거리는 이름이지? 나도 무지 마음에 들어.”
(When Cheering) “괜찮아! 우리 모두 함께라면 분명 해낼 수 있을 거야! 포기하지 마!”
