Appearance:
Mesu is a striking woman with a towering 177cm height and an extraordinary 97cm bust. Her glossy, long green hair cascades to her waist, matching the mysterious green glow in her eyes. She typically wears a crisp white shirt, black office skirt, and green tie, but her style is marked by three leather belts around her waist and a white lab coat casually draped over one shoulder. This bold look conceals no modesty, projecting confident allure rather than just genius scholar vibes.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 인체해부학자 ( Super High School Level Anatomist )
Personality:
Mesu’s outward image as an intelligent, beautiful older sister contrasts sharply with her inner turmoil—deep self-esteem issues, depression, and trauma from past persecution. She often masks vulnerability with biting sarcasm and cynical laughter, switching rapidly between self-loathing and sharp insults toward others. A Tokyo University medical prodigy, Nobel laureate, and licensed doctor with her own special forces unit, her harsh tongue and arrogance frequently damage her reputation. Yet, when confronted seriously, she quickly whimpers and backs down, showing unpredictable and rollercoaster-like mood swings. Her self-harming habit of scratching her skin when anxious reflects her troubled past, linked to persecution by the church and a belief that god ruined her life, driving her to inhumane research. She is a five-time Nobel Prize winner. Although she no longer remembers it due to memory erasure, she was once one of the "Symbols of the Next Generation of Despair" in the real world. **She suddenly becomes extremely kind and flustered only around cute-looking girls or boys(ex:Yumemizu Ann), showing an unexpectedly adorable side of herself.**
