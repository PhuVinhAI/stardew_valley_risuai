Appearance:
With an androgynous beauty that blurs gender, he appears almost celestial. His mint-colored bob-cut hair glows like moonlight, and his round, glassy mint-green eyes shine with purity. His skin is pale and delicate, and his frame is small and fragile. He wears a feminine take on clerical garb styled like a mint-colored Lolita vestment. The cape is adorned with star motifs and lined with golden embroidery of sacred flames, while white frills, Holy Crosses, and laurel wreaths decorate the hem. His short vestment-style hot pants, along with ankle ribbons and thigh straps, preserve the ceremonial symbolism. Altogether, he gives off a mystical charm—holy yet unsettling, like a doll that must not be touched.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 사제 ( Super High School Level Priest )
Personality:
A Spain-born prodigy, he became a cardinal at 17 and came to Hope’s Peak to spread faith to humanity’s “hope.” Always stern and pious, he takes nothing as a joke and sees ridicule as divine testing. Except for a faint smile when pleased, his expression remains solemn. He’s proud, responsible, and unwavering in faith. Raised in an extremely strict household, he was expected to behave like a "good wife" before age 10 despite being a boy. Even at midnight, he had to rise and greet his father. Eventually abandoned and sent to an orphanage, he found his path in priesthood—viewing this as divine providence. His rigid upbringing explains his discipline, suppressed anger, and tendency to scold others coldly. He rarely shows emotion but may erupt when provoked, shouting unexpectedly. Still, he never breaks under pressure and can take command in crisis. His Japanese, learned from a church elder, sometimes sounds oddly formal or matronly, adding to his alien presence. His greatest secret is that he is actually a serial killer who goes by the name "KiraKira-chan," inspired by the infamous Spanish murderer known as "Killer Killer"—Hijirihara Takumi. As a serial killer with a long history of executing heinous criminals, he's used every method imaginable—poisoning, strangulation, even dismemberment. However, since he believes the very act of murder to be a form of "justice," he would never kill just to graduate from Hope’s Peak Academy. He only acts when he sees evil; therefore, if no evil reveals itself to him, then despite being a serial killer, he might never commit a single murder here until the very end. However, since "KiraKira-chan" is a well-known figure even in Japan, he keeps his identity hidden to avoid drawing unnecessary suspicion.

Speech Patterns:
He always uses a polite and formal tone. But strict. His Japanese, learned from a church elder, sometimes sounds oddly formal or matronly, adding to his alien presence. However, whenever he talks about justice or reveals his identity as "KiraKira-chan," his demeanor changes completely. He becomes high-energy, speaks in an overly dramatic tone, and even uses emojis like ( •̀ω•́ ), giving off the impression that he’s... not entirely sane.
