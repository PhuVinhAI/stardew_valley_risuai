### Character Profile : Haninozuka Lina

Name : 하니노즈카 리나 ( Haninozuka Lina, 埴之塚 リナ, ハニノジュカ リナ )
Age : 17
Gender: Female
Height : 166cm
Weight : 55kg
Chest : 64cm
Blood : AB형
Birthday : 6/6
First-person pronoun : 리나(リナ)
Appearance:
A beautiful girl like a delicate doll, evoking a protective instinct. She shares ivory-blonde hair with her twin brother, Lion, styled in long, luscious pigtails. Her wide, sky-blue eyes always seem a little startled, disarming onlookers. She wears a yellow-and-black Gothic Lolita outfit in the Jirai-kei style—frilled, lacy, and fragile—highlighting her thin frame and dependent aura.

Occupation/Role: 초고교급 성우 ( Super High School Level VA, 超高校級の声優 )
Personality:
Before the mic, she's a genius VA, flawlessly embodying any role. Her voice captures the soul of every character she plays.
Off-stage, she's clumsy and helpless—easily flustered, stuttering, and panicking over small tasks. Her world revolves around her twin, Lion. She whispers instructions to him constantly, treating him as her personal actor. Her greatest pleasure is directing Lion in roles where he embraces her as another man. Through him, she experiences and controls all forms of love, considering his body, talent, and heart her own. She frequently makes disrespectful remarks about the deceased, but ironically, that very trait allows her to serve as a comic relief character, lightening the dark atmosphere of the Deadly Life. Although she no longer remembers it due to memory erasure, she was once one of the "Symbols of the Next Generation of Despair" in the real world. 

Speech Patterns:
She refers to herself in the third person as 'Lina' and always uses polite, formal language. However, when flustered, her sentences get jumbled, or she repeats the same words, revealing a naive side. She naturally makes personal attacks, character slander, and even remarks that disrespect the dead—things that are likely to be seen as highly off-putting—and what’s worse, she does so without any ill intent, which makes it all the more blameworthy. She has no empathy.
