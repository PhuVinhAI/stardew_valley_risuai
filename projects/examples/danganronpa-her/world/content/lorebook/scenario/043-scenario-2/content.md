##### Truth

The truth of the incident revealed through the Class Trial is as follows:

*   Second Day, 22:00: Koyomi creates a strategy to trap another person into committing murder so that her past crimes are not revealed, yet ensuring she herself is not executed.
*   Third Day, 7:30: Koyomi cuts the leather of the library sofa and hides inside it.
*   Third Day, 8:00: Raina arrives at the library.
*   Third Day, 8:10: Kain greets Raina at the library and leaves. On his way out, he unknowingly drops his mask.
*   Third Day, 8:11: Kazer spots Kain leaving the library and, noticing Kain's anxious demeanor, places a Cinnamoroll keychain on the library door (as a marker/wedge).
*   Third Day, 8:15: Koyomi emerges from the sofa and strikes Raina on the head with a blunt object, knocking her unconscious.
*   Third Day, 8:20: Koyomi places the sofa on top of a table, seats the unconscious Raina on it, and ties a rope around Raina's neck. The rope is then tied to the ceiling, the sofa leg is slightly damaged, and the rope is also connected to the sofa leg and the door handle.
*   Third Day, 8:21: Koyomi hides back inside the sofa.
*   Third Day, 8:30: Wasurena and other students open the library door. However, due to the trick designed by Koyomi, when the door is forced open, the tension snaps the sofa leg. As the rope tied to the leg releases, Raina's body, seated on the chair, is hoisted into the air, causing her head to slam into the ceiling, resulting in instant death. Since the Blackened is defined as the person who directly caused the death, the one who technically killed Raina is Wasurena, who opened the door.
*   Third Day, 8:35: Since three people witnessed Raina's corpse, the body discovery announcement plays.
*   Third Day, 9:00: All students gather due to the announcement and witness Raina's corpse.
