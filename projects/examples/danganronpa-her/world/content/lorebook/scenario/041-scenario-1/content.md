The truth of the incident revealed through the Class Trial is as follows:

*   Second Day, 22:00: Star is provoked by the Motive DVD and decides to commit murder.
*   Second Day, 22:30: Star goes to the gymnasium to acquire the weapon, the 'gold-leafed awl (needle)'. However, Yumemizu and Mesu were there, so she needed to be deceptive. Star hides the 'gold-leafed awl (needle)' in her dress and smuggles it out. Many people besides Star entered and exited the gym.
*   Second Day, 23:00: Saya plans a party for his friends who are depressed due to the Motive DVDs.
*   Second Day, 23:00: Saya hosts the party. All students participate.
*   Second Day, 23:10: All friends gather at the Hall, the party venue. Some students (Wasurena, Kain, Mesu, Kazer, Star, Koyomi, Yumemizu) drink alcohol at this time.
*   Second Day, 23:15: During the party, Star secretly stabs Saya in the chest from behind with the thin needle. Because the wound was small enough to induce excessive bleeding unnoticed, Saya did not realize he had been stabbed. Since no one noticed, the party continued.
*   Second Day, 23:30: Blood continued to flow from the small wound on Saya's chest, but the Obi (sash) he wore under his furisode was wrapped tightly around the wound, acting as a tourniquet. Therefore, neither Saya nor anyone else noticed the wound or bleeding.
*   Second Day, 23:55: Saya started to stagger due to significant blood loss, but neither Saya nor anyone present noticed the true cause.
*   Third Day, 0:00: The party ends, and everyone returns to their private rooms.
*   Third Day, 0:05: The staggering Saya untied the tight Obi only after the party ended, realizing too late that something was seriously wrong, and soon lost consciousness. There had been little bleeding due to the Obi, but severe hemorrhaging began the moment it was released.
*   Third Day, 0:10: Wasurena, heading to the changing room, discovers the dying Saya with his upper garment removed.
*   Third Day, 0:15: Wasurena urgently informs his most trusted ally, Kain, of Saya's condition, and the dying Saya is belatedly moved to the nurse's office by the two.
*   Third Day, 0:20: Wasurena and Kain urgently call Mesu, the expert, for medical treatment, but Mesu is unable to move as she is vomiting in the restroom, dead drunk.
