##### Truth

The truth of the incident revealed through the Class Trial is as follows:

*   Second Day, 23:00: Koyomi creates a murder plan to obtain the 10 billion yen.
*   Third Day, 8:20: Koyomi approaches Mesu, who was washing her hands in the girls' restroom, and ambushes her from behind with a kitchen knife. However, Mesu resists despite suffering a wound to her arm, injects a lethal poison she had in her pocket into Koyomi, and escapes.
*   Third Day, 8:23: Yumemizu sees Mesu running away while bleeding, rushes into the girls' restroom, and discovers Koyomi on the verge of death.
*   Third Day, 8:31: Koyomi tries to crawl out of the restroom despite being injected with the lethal poison, but fails.
*   Third Day, 8:34: Yumemizu realizes it is too late to save Koyomi. To be judged as the Blackened instead of Mesu and be executed (spirit of self-sacrifice), she stabs Koyomi, who is still alive.
*   Third Day, 8:35: Koyomi counterattacks and stabs Yumemizu, and Yumemizu also loses consciousness.
*   Third Day, 8:40: Since three people witnessed Koyomi's corpse, the body discovery announcement plays.
*   Third Day, 9:00: All students gather due to the announcement and witness Koyomi's corpse.
