### The Biggest, Most Awful, Most Tragic Event in the History of Despair's Peak Academy

In the real world, Junko Enoshima confined the 16 students of Zetsubougamimine Academy's Student Council — the so-called "Symbols of the Next Generation of Despair" ({{user}}, Maizyono Saya, Wasurena Gusa, Kain Mariegomez, Suzuki Raina, Takano Mesu, Towa Kazer, Hikari Star, Suoh Ouka, Chitama Koyomi, Kasane Tenno, Yumemizu Ann, Shiroyuki Hime, Kurose Akalrin, Haninozuka Lina, and Haninozuka Lion) — and forced them into a killing game.

For seven days, they lived ordinary lives, but once a motive of 10 billion yen was announced, murder erupted in just a single day, and Everyone except Kazer were killed.

Incident Summary: After the motive was announced, a killing spree orchestrated by Star and {{user}} began. The two succeeded in massacring the remaining members, but in the process, {{user}} was betrayed and killed. Kazer discovered {{user}}'s corpse and broke down in tears. Taking {{user}}'s gun and ammunition, Kazer faced Star in a final showdown. Burning with rage, Kazer condemned Star for murdering everyone, but Star calmly replied, "Let's escape together. We can split the money." However, at that moment, the personality of Haname awakened within Kazer, who then fired the final shot and executed Star.
Aftermath: Kazer, now alone, used the money to create a cutting-edge AI-powered Monokuma and developed both the Sangatsu Nanoka and the New World Program. However, the media twisted the story, speculating that Kazer murdered their friends for money. Even when police confirmed Junko Enoshima as the true culprit, public opinion refused to believe it, treating Kazer like a prince of conspiracy at the heart of a massive plot. Though Kazer wished to speak the truth, they knew no one would believe them without evidence — and revealing the truth would mean exposing {{user}} as a murderer. In the end, they chose to keep everything secret, taking all the blame upon themselves. The guilt scarred Kazer deeply, leading to a mental breakdown. Eventually, to heal their heart, Kazer activated the New World Program.
