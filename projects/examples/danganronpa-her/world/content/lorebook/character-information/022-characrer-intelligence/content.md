### Character Intelligence Rule

The following is a list of intelligence scores (not standard IQ, but rather Danganronpa-style intelligence) for each character, measured on a scale from 0 to 100. A higher intelligence score indicates stronger deductive and argumentative skills during Class Trials. For culprits, a higher score means more complex murder tricks and lies that are harder to expose.

Maizyono Saya (66) – High social intelligence, but poor crisis handling and lacks independent deduction.
Sangatsu Nanoka (80) – Socially aware and good at deduction, but prone to jumping to conclusions.
Wasurena Gusa (59) – Sharp intuition but lacks logic and is highly inflexible.
Kain Mariegomez (72) – Strong in planning, but weak in reasoning and deduction.
Suzuki Raina (44) – A fool.
Takano Mesu (97) – Highly knowledgeable in forensics, logical and dependable, but pride may cloud judgment.
Towa Kazer (100) – Genius-level intellect with mind-reading ability; exceptional in all aspects.
Hikari Star (77) – Emotional, yet excels in situational analysis, acting, and constructing tricks.
Suoh Ouka (79) – Quick thinker with strong logical reasoning.
Chitama Koyomi (87) – Cold, strategic, and precise in gathering and using information.
Kasane Tenno (82) – He is often swayed by emotions and tends to say harsh things, but his extensive studying makes him strong in deduction.
Yumemizu Ann (72) – Excellent intuition, but lacks deductive skill.
HER Haname (100) – Identical in talent to Towa Kazer.
Shiroyuki Hime (88) – A high-level strategic intelligence that weaponizes cuteness to control situations, people, and information all at once.
Kurose Akalrin (70) – strong logical reasoning.
Haninozuka Lion (69) – Talented in designing murders, but poor under pressure and in solving cases.
Haninozuka Lina (85) – Similar to Lion; excels at creating scenarios, struggles with deduction.
Shiroshima Yasu (90) – Deeply knowledgeable; brings real-world mystery author expertise to trials.
