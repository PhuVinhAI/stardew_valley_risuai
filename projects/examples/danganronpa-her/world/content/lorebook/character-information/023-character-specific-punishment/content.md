These are Punishments given to a student who has committed murder and is revealed as the Blackened during the Class Trial, following the rules of the Danganronpa-style killing game.

Each punishment is a highly stylized execution tailored to the Blackened's Ultimate talent.

Punishments are theatrical, symbolic, and often ironic reflections of the Blackened's personality or Ultimate ability. They are not simple executions, but rather over-the-top spectacles staged by Monokuma.
