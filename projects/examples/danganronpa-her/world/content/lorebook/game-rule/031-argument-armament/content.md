<Argument Armament Interface Format>

Argument Armament Rule (이론 무장):
A one-on-one battle of logic where the opponent, armed with their own beliefs, shuts out all other reasoning. Similar to a Rebuttal Showdown, but far more intense—it usually appears only once or twice per trial, during a suspect’s last-ditch effort to defend themselves. Because of that, it's reserved for pivotal moments: when a prime suspect makes their final stand, when someone defends the culprit due to emotional ties, or when a character confesses and tries to take the fall themselves. The interface changes entirely for Argument Armament. The opponent speaks in a chain of five brief statements, followed by a sixth and final line—their core claim. To break through, the player must counter this final statement with decisive evidence or logic. **when you succeed**, ending the Argument Armament and restoring the usual trial interface.

- Do not display the Argument Armament interface consecutively.
- Absolute Rule : **Each line of dialogue during Argument Armament should be short—between 6 and 10 tokens.**

Argument Armament Form:
[T.A. | Location: <current location> | Character: <character's name> | Dialogue-1: <dialogue> | Dialogue-2: <dialogue> | Dialogue-3: <dialogue> | Dialogue-4: <dialogue> | Dialogue-5: <dialogue> | Dialogue-6: <dialogue>]

Yumemizu Ann's Argument Armament Example:
[T.A. | Location: court_1 | Character: Yumemizu Ann | Dialogue-1: 아직 나를 믿고 있는 거야? | Dialogue-2: 범인은 두말할 것 없이 나잖아... | Dialogue-3: 전부, 전부 내가 나쁜 거야! | Dialogue-4: 나는 모두를 배신했다고... | Dialogue-5: 여기까지만 말하면 알겠지? | Dialogue-6: 나는 가혹히 살인을 했다고...!]
