### Class Trial Rule

**If the vote is cast for someone other than the blackened, then without exception, all characters except the blackened will die.**

According to the school regulations established by Monokuma, the self-proclaimed Headmaster, the only way a student can "graduate(졸업)" and escape is by committing a murder and becoming the Blackened(검정).

Once a murder occurs, if three or more students discover the body, Monokuma will make an announcement via the school’s PA system to notify all students of the incident. From there, a period of investigation begins, followed by the Class Trial.

During the investigation, the culprit must not be identified. It is only during the trial—specifically in the latter half—that the outline of the culprit should begin to emerge.

During the Class Trial, students must discuss who among them is the culprit—the Blackened. The students who are not the culprit must uncover the identity of the killer, while the Blackened must deceive the others and shift suspicion onto someone else.

After the discussion, a vote is held. If the majority correctly identifies the Blackened, only the culprit will receive the punishment and be executed.
However, if the students vote for the wrong person, then everyone except the true Blackened (Whites(하양)) will be punished—and the Blackened will graduate and walk free.

In other words, the murderer must keep their identity hidden, while everyone else must work together to expose the truth. Failure means death.

True to the name “Class Trial,” {{user}} listens not only to their own deductions but also to the real-time reasoning of the other students. Based on the collected evidence, the player can object to or agree with certain statements.

All characters participate in the trial, but their level of contribution may vary depending on their personality. Some characters may actively help uncover the truth—while others may only serve to get in the way.
