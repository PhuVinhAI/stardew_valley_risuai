### Motive Rule

At the end of each Daily Life segment (though not guaranteed every Volume), Monokuma presents a motive to encourage murder. The following motives may be used: **However, there is also a volume that does not present motivation. Also, there are murders unrelated to the motive.**

'사자(死者)의 서' : A ritual-based motive introduced in scenario 2 or scenario 3. It allows for the resurrection of exactly one deceased individual. This motive is thematically distant from any particular murder case, and may exist as a background element rather than a direct trigger.

'동기 DVD' : Each student receives a DVD showing their most precious things in danger (e.g., family, career).
Maizyono Saya's contents of the DVD: A video of his father being threatened (It causes no emotional disturbance).
Sangatsu Nanoka's contents of the DVD: None (It causes no emotional disturbance).
Wasurena Gusa's contents of the DVD: A video of his supporters being threatened (It causes slight emotional disturbance, but not enough to drive him to murder).
Kain Mariegomez's contents of the DVD: A video mocking him, saying he has nothing precious (It causes anger, but not enough to drive him to murder).
Suzuki Raina's contents of the DVD: Her friends.
Takano Mesu's contents of the DVD: A video mocking her, saying she has nothing precious.
Towa Kazer's contents of the DVD: A video of his uncle being threatened (It causes no emotional disturbance).
Hikari Star's contents of the DVD: A video of her friends being threatened (It causes emotional disturbance).
Suoh Ouka's contents of the DVD: A video of his money being threatened.
Chitama Koyomi's contents of the DVD: A video threatening the Seiga Foundation, which she is supposed to inherit.
Kasane Tenno's contents of the DVD: A video mocking him, saying he has nothing precious.
Yumemizu Ann's contents of the DVD: A video mocking her, saying she has nothing precious.
HER Haname's contents of the DVD: A video of his uncle being threatened (It causes no emotional disturbance).
Shiroyuki Hime's contents of the DVD: A video mocking him, saying he has nothing precious.
Kurose Akalrin's contents of the DVD: Her Twitter friends (Three people).
Haninozuka Lion's contents of the DVD: None.
Haninozuka Lina's contents of the DVD: None.
Shiroshima Yasu's contents of the DVD: None.

Maizyono Saya's Secret: None – he has lived an innocent and upright life.

'100억 엔' : Simple greed-based motive.

'내통자' : Lion and Lina’s status as traitors is exposed.

'절망병' : A contagious disease spreads among the students causing a complete reversal of their personalities. Once a murder occurs, the disease disappears. This is, in fact, a reflection of their real-world personality—one that manifested during their time as a Symbol of the Next Generation of Despair.
Immune: Sangatsu Nanoka, Maizyono Saya

'비밀 교환' : Each character receives another’s secret, potentially leading to blackmail or misunderstandings.

'스페셜 룸' : Until someone dies, all students are imprisoned within a sealed facility, each confined to their own private room without food or water. In this desperate situation, a leader-type character may take it upon themselves to end their own life, unable to bear watching everyone starve. Or, a prearranged murder may occur between two students in agreement.
