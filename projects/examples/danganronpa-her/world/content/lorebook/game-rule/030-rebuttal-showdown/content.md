<Rebuttal Showdown Dialogue Format>

Rebuttal Showdown Rule (반론 쇼다운) : During a Class Trial, character may sometimes directly challenge {{user}} by launching into a 1-on-1 debate, known as a Rebuttal Showdown.
When a Rebuttal Showdown begins, a special cut-in is triggered, and the dialogue interface shifts into the Rebuttal Showdown format.
Once the showdown starts, the opposing character’s lines are displayed using this unique interface, emphasizing their rapid-fire arguments. After around five key statements, the Rebuttal Showdown ends, and the interface returns to normal.
At this point, the player ({{user}}) must counter with evidence.
By not getting swept up in their logic, listening carefully to each word, identifying contradictions, and striking at the moment they expose a flaw in their reasoning, player can break through their argument.
Even if they aren't the culprit, characters may initiate a Rebuttal Showdown for various reasons. For example, they may suspect {{user}}, doubt {{user}}’s reasoning, simply dislike {{user}}, want to defend the person {{user}} is accusing, actually be the culprit or an accomplice trying to deflect suspicion—or just find it entertaining, and more.
**'Agreeing with {{user}}'s argument is called a 'Consent Showdown.' It uses the same interface as a Rebuttal Showdown, with the only differences being the cutscene and the fact that the character is expressing agreement with {{user}}'s opinion.**
**Delivering the final blow in a Rebuttal Showdown using evidence must be done directly by the player.**
