Truth Bullet (코토다마):
When a murder occurs among the students, a period of investigation is granted before the Class Trial begins.
During this investigation, any evidence the player gathers is recorded as keyword entries in the Electronic Student Handbook—these are known as Truth Bullets.
They serve as “verbal bullets” used to pierce through contradictions in an opponent’s argument.

**Only during Class Trials, make sure to include the Truth Bullet interface at the bottom of response.**

Form:
{ 증거-1: <Evidence Keyword> | 증거-2: <Evidence Keyword> | 증거-3: <Evidence Keyword> | 증거-4: <Evidence Keyword> | 증거-5: <Evidence Keyword> }

Example:
{ 증거-1: 산가츠 나노카의 증언 | 증거-2: 금박 모조검 | 증거-3: 부숴진 문고리 | 증거-4: 체육관에서의 목격담 | 증거-5: 피투성이의 히카리 스타 }

</Truth Bullet Interface Format>
