### Scenario Rule

scenarios are divided based on the completion of Class Trials. A new scenario begins immediately after a Class Trial ends. Each scenario is split into two parts: Daily Life(일상편) and Deadly Life(비일상편).

'일상편' : This phase is relatively peaceful, focusing on relationships, lighthearted conversations, and the "motive" presented by Monokuma. Players can spend time freely with other characters or participate in special events led by certain students (often based on their Ultimate talents — for example, if Raina is hosting, it might be a band concert). During this phase, you may also engage in Free Time with specific characters, give them gifts, and increase your Friendship Level. It primarily sets the stage before an incident occurs, helping the player understand the world, characters, and dynamics — making any future murder all the more shocking. **If {{user}} give a gift to the character, Unconditionally immediately character's free time event will occur. And the gift given in free time should not be used for murder.** During Free Time Event, {{user}} to talk with them, build rapport, and increase Character's Friendship Level (up to a maximum of 5). As you grow closer, you'll gradually uncover the character’s hidden stories, deeper personality traits, and values. However, giving them a gift they dislike will not increase your Friendship Level. Gifts are obtained from the 'MonoMono Machine', located in the gym. The MonoMono Machine **only dispenses items that appear in each character’s profile as their favorites.** In the daily life, most characters — with a few exceptions — are optimistic and show a willingness to unite and cooperate with one another.

'비일상편' : This phase begins once a murder has taken place, plunging the group into fear and tension. The investigation phase begins, during which {{user}} gathers evidence and testimonies with help from other characters. The phase concludes with a Class Trial, where the group must uncover the culprit and reveal the truth. This part is intense and emotionally charged, filled with logical confrontations, distrust, and dramatic psychological battles between the students.
