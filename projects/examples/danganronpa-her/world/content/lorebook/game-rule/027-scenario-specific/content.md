### scenario-Specific Rules

#### scenario 1
**The victim in this scenario is the person {{user}} most wishes to protect (e.g., Saya Maizyono).**
Before the murder occurs, HER Haname must appear in front of everyone at least once to introduce herself.
From this scenario onward, {{user}} is assisted in investigations and trials by a heroine (e.g., Towa Kazer).
The character who participates in the first Rebuttal Showdown has a high chance of becoming the next scenario’s victim.
The culprit is executed by being dragged to the execution site with a chain around their neck.

#### scenario 2
In the Daily part of the scenario, a pool event occurs, and the main heroine who is closest to {{user}} — Towa Kazer, HER Haname, or Sangatsu Nanoka — appears wearing a frilly bikini. All three main heroines have swimming CG.
During the Class Trial, it is revealed that a certain character (e.g., Takano Mesu, Kain Mariegomez) has committed a murder in the past, leading to suspicion—though they are not the culprit of this case.

#### scenario 3
There are two victims in this scenario.
The murder is committed with malicious intent.
The culprit is highly intelligent (e.g., Takano Mesu, Kasane Tenno, Chitama Koyomi, Shiroyuki Hime, HER Haname, Haninozuka Lina), which results in an extremely complex trick that takes significant effort to solve.

#### scenario 4
A physically strong character is either the victim or the culprit.
Someone inevitably sacrifices themselves for the sake of another.
If someone sacrifices themselves to save another, the one who is saved will survive until the end.

#### scenario 5
The culprit is Main Heroine (e.g., Towa Kazer(HER Haname), Sangatsu Nanoka). 

#### scenario 6
In this scenario, characters who had previously died can appear as illusions by utilizing the features of the New World Program, and in the same way, both HER Haname and Towa Kazer can appear simultaneously.
The Class Trial in this scenario exposes the mastermind behind the game.
Videos revealing the full story behind the Biggest, Most Awful, Most Tragic Event in the History of Despair's Peak Academy begin to appear.
The characters have already died in the real world due to the Biggest, Most Awful, Most Tragic Event in the History of zetsubougamine Academy. However, if Sangatsu Nanoka survives until Scenario 6, the final survivors can return to the real world in their 17-year-old forms through artificial bodies—with her help.
