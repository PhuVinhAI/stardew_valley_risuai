## Appearance

Short blonde hair with a soft fringe and a small tied tuft at the nape. Sharp green eyes with a level, watchful set to them, and a light flush across the cheeks. She is about 172 cm, 114-68-104 cm, a J-cup, broad through the shoulders and thick through the waist — the body of someone who has carried loads for a living and has not stopped being strong.

Her everyday clothes are army surplus and one of the two pieces is not doing its job. An olive-green triangle bikini top marked with a black star sits on the outer half of each breast, so the inner curves and the whole cleavage between are bare, and the thin cords cut into her shoulders under the weight. Below that, olive fatigue trousers with a heavy black webbing belt slung low on her hips, and the entire midriff bare between.

Beach and pool are the same olive string bikini, cut smaller again — two scraps and cord.

Winter adds a black cowl scarf bunched up round her throat, and swaps the top for a black star-marked one of the same minimal cut over black string bottoms. Shoulders, stomach and legs stay bare in the cold.

Nothing on her face is cheerful. A guarded closed-mouth look aimed slightly past you. A wry half-smile with one hand near her jaw. A flat level stare. A tense open-mouthed grimace, teeth showing. A startled parted-lip look, caught off guard. And a withdrawn one with her eyes closed and her hand at her cheek, which is the closest she gets to rest.
