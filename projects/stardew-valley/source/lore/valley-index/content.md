# Places, Festivals, and Households

Names in this index, in either language, pull the full entry for that subject into the next reply. Use them.

## In town

Pelican Town — the town square, the notice board, the graveyard. *Quảng trường, bảng tin, nghĩa trang.*

Pierre's General Store — seeds and groceries; the family lives above it. *Cửa hàng tổng hợp.*

Stardrop Saloon — the bar, the pool table, the dish of the day. *Quán rượu.*

The Blacksmith — Clint's forge, tool upgrades, geodes. *Tiệm rèn.*

Harvey's Clinic — the valley's only doctor. *Phòng khám.*

Museum and Library — Gunther's collection and reading room. *Bảo tàng, thư viện.*

Mayor's Manor — Lewis's house and the town records. *Dinh thị trưởng.*

Community Center — the ruined hall and the Junimos. *Nhà văn hóa.*

JojaMart and the Cinema — the warehouse store and the movie theatre. *Siêu thị Joja, rạp chiếu phim.*

Willow Lane — Jodi's house and the two sisters' house. *Đường Willow.*

River Road — George and Evelyn's house, Pam's trailer. *Đường ven sông.*

## Beyond the square

The Farm — the old farmhouse, the fields, the shipping bin. *Nông trại.*

The Mountain — the mountain road, the lake, the quarry. *Ngọn núi.*

Carpenter's Shop — Robin's workshop and the mountain house. *Tiệm thợ mộc.*

The Mines — the shafts, the lift, the levels below. *Hầm mỏ.*

Adventurer's Guild — Marlon's weapons and the bounty board. *Hội mạo hiểm.*

Railroad and Spa — the tracks, the bath house, the hot spring. *Đường sắt, suối nước nóng.*

Cindersap Forest — the woods south of town, the river, the forest lake. *Rừng Cindersap.*

Secret Woods — the hidden clearing in the old growth. *Rừng bí mật.*

Marnie's Ranch — the animal shop and the livestock barn. *Trại chăn nuôi.*

Forest Cottages — Leah's cabin, Elliott's cabin on the shore. *Nhà gỗ của Leah.*

Wizard's Tower and the Sewers — the tower at the forest's western edge. *Tòa tháp, cống ngầm.*

Pelican Beach — the shore, the pier, the tide pools. *Bãi biển.*

Fish Shop and Pier — Willy's shop, bait and tackle. *Tiệm cá, cầu tàu.*

Bus Stop and Backwoods — the broken bus and the road out. *Trạm xe buýt.*

Calico Desert — the sand, the oasis, Sandy's shop, Skull Cavern. *Sa mạc.*

Ginger Island — the boat south, the volcano, the resort. *Hòn đảo.*

Valley Geography — how far anything is from anything else. *Bản đồ thung lũng.*

## The festival calendar

Spring — Egg Festival (13), Desert Festival (15–17), Flower Dance (24). *Lễ hội trứng, lễ hội sa mạc, lễ hội hoa.*

Summer — Luau (11), Trout Derby (20–21), Dance of the Moonlight Jellies (28). *Tiệc Luau, giải câu cá, đêm sứa.*

Autumn — Stardew Valley Fair (16), Spirit's Eve (27). *Hội chợ thung lũng, đêm ma quái.*

Winter — Festival of Ice (8), SquidFest (12–13), Night Market (15–17), Feast of the Winter Star (25). *Lễ hội băng, lễ hội mực, chợ đêm, tiệc sao mùa đông.*

Emily and Haley — the two sisters sharing a house on Willow Lane. *Hai chị em.*

Pam and Penny — mother and daughter in the River Road trailer. *Mẹ và con gái.*

Marnie and Shane — the ranch household, aunt and niece. *Gia đình ở trang trại.*

Lewis and Marnie — the relationship neither of them announces. *Quan hệ bí mật.*

Caroline and the Wizard — the old path and what happened on it. *Chuyện cũ trong rừng.*
