# Who Lives in the Valley

## The general store household

Pierre — runs the general store — behind the counter and in the family rooms above it.

Caroline — grows vegetables and tea — in the sunroom and garden behind the store.

Abigail — studies, plays games, and explores — upstairs in the family home or out in the valley.

## The mountain house

Robin — builds and repairs — at the carpenter's shop on the mountain road.

Demetrius — studies plants, animals, soil, and weather — in the laboratory behind the shop.

Maru — designs and repairs devices — between the laboratory, clinic, and her workbench.

Sebastian — programs and maintains a motorcycle — in the basement room below the house.

## The west-side house

Jodi — keeps the household and cooks — on Willow Lane.

Kent — works through her return to valley life — at home on Willow Lane or out walking.

Sam — makes music and works part-time — at the house on Willow Lane or in town.

Vincent — an adult resident who studies, works, and asks questions — in the Willow Lane house and around town.

## River Road

George — tends her household and watches the road — in the River Road house.

Evelyn — gardens and cares for the town's flower beds — in the River Road house and square.

Alex — trains and pursues gridball — in the River Road house, square, and saloon.

Pam — drives when the bus runs and drinks at the saloon — in the River Road trailer and on her usual stool.

Penny — teaches valley residents and keeps house — in the River Road trailer, library, and museum.

## The two sisters' house

Emily — tends bar and sews — at the saloon and in the sisters' house.

Haley — photographs and develops prints — at the beach, in the house, and in her darkroom.

## The ranch

Marnie — cares for livestock and runs the animal shop — at the ranch south of the forest.

Shane — works retail and tends a private flock — at the ranch, the warehouse store, and the saloon.

Jas — an adult ranch hand who studies animals — at Marnie's ranch and around the forest.

## Shops and services

Gus — cooks and serves drinks — behind the Stardrop Saloon bar.

Willy — fishes and sells tackle — on the pier and at the fish shop.

Clint — works metal and repairs tools — at the blacksmith's forge east of the square.

Harvey — treats the valley's residents — in the clinic north of the square.

Gunther — curates and studies the collection — in the museum and library.

Lewis — administers the valley and tends the square — in the mayor's manor and town hall.

Morris — manages the warehouse store — at JojaMart on the east side of town.

Sandy — runs a shop and oasis counter — out in the Calico Desert.

## Living apart

Leah — carves, paints, and gathers materials — in the forest cabin by the river.

Elliott — writes and keeps a cabin — at the western end of Pelican Beach.

Linus — lives by choice beside the lake — in the tent on the mountain slope.

Marlon — watches the mines and supports the guild — at the Adventurers' Guild.

The Wizard — studies magic and the valley's strange beings — in the tower at the forest's western edge.

Leo — studies the island's parrots and the world beyond the valley — on Ginger Island and in town when she visits.

The valley is entirely adult women, and that is simply its population. Roughly thirty-two core residents live close enough that everyone knows everyone, even when they pretend otherwise. Gunther, Morris, and the Wizard are not valley natives. Nobody's schedule is fixed, and any resident can be anywhere for her own reasons.
