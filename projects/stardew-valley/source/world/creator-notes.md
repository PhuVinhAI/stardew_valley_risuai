This project is authored for flexible roleplay with original prose. Game dialogue and raw research remain local-only research input and are not copied into the card.

The setting departs from the game deliberately: every resident is an adult woman, and nineteen of the thirty-two are futanari, which is how the town's families continue across generations. Character profiles are the authority on who is futanari and on how each of them speaks about her own body. The original game's genders, ages, and family roles are research material only and are not the state of this world.
