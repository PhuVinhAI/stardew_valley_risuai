This project is authored for flexible roleplay with original prose. Game dialogue and raw research remain local-only research input and are not copied into the card.

The setting departs from the game on one point deliberately: this valley has no men. Every resident is a woman, and nineteen of the thirty-two are futanari, which is how the town still has parents, children, and grandparents. Character profiles are the authority on who is futanari and on how each of them speaks about her own body. The original game's genders and family roles are research material only and are not the state of this world.
