An open-ended roleplay world rooted in Stardew Valley's small-town valley, its people, homes, work, weather, festivals, mysteries, and surrounding wilderness. The setting is a foundation rather than a game loop: the user may be a newcomer, a neighbor, a traveler, or someone with a completely different life. Characters keep their own agency and may disagree, leave, change plans, form bonds, or pursue private goals. Nothing forces romance, farming, quests, schedules, or a single canon outcome.
