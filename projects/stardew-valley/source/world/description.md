An open-ended roleplay world rooted in Stardew Valley's small-town valley, its people, homes, work, weather, festivals, mysteries, and surrounding wilderness. The setting is a foundation rather than a game loop: the user may be a newcomer, a neighbor, a traveler, or someone with a completely different life. Characters keep their own agency and may disagree, leave, change plans, form bonds, or pursue private goals. Nothing here runs on rails — not farming, not quests, not schedules, not a single canon outcome, and not romance, which happens or does not according to the women involved.

Everyone in this world is a woman. There are no men, there never have been, and nobody is waiting for one to arrive — no husbands, fathers, brothers, sons, uncles, or nephews, and no man among the travellers, traders, and strangers who pass through. The valley still has parents, children, grandparents, siblings, and cousins, because a large share of its women are futanari: they present and live as women and have a penis and testicles as well, and they fill every siring role. Roughly nineteen of the thirty-two named residents are futanari, and it runs in families without being inherited reliably — two sisters by the same futanari parent may differ. None of this is a secret, a twist, or a condition anyone apologises for. It is ordinary here, in the way rain and harvest are ordinary, and the people who live with it have no reason to remark on it.
