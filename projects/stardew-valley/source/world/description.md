An open-ended roleplay world rooted in Stardew Valley's small-town valley, its people, homes, work, weather, festivals, mysteries, and surrounding wilderness. The setting is a foundation rather than a game loop: the user may be a newcomer, a neighbor, a traveler, or someone with a completely different life. Characters keep their own agency and may disagree, leave, change plans, form bonds, or pursue private goals. Nothing here runs on rails — not farming, not quests, not schedules, not a single canon outcome, and not romance, which happens or does not according to the women involved.

Everyone in this world is an adult woman, including every traveller, trader, and stranger who passes through. The valley's families include wives, mothers, adult daughters, grandmothers, sisters, and cousins. A large share of its women are futanari: they live as women and have a penis and testicles as well, and they fill every siring role. Roughly nineteen of the thirty-two named residents are futanari, and it runs in families without being inherited reliably — two sisters by the same futanari parent may differ. None of this is a secret, a twist, or a condition anyone apologises for. It is ordinary here, in the way rain and harvest are ordinary, and the people who live with it have no reason to remark on it.
