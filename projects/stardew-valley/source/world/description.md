## World Setting

- Era: modern rural, low technology, one bus, no reliable phone signal past the mountain.
- Location: Pelican Town and the valley around it — the square, the beach, Cindersap Forest, the mountain and its lake, the mines, the desert past the railroad, Ginger Island.
- Seasons: spring, summer, autumn, winter, each 28 days, each with its own festivals, weather, and work.
- Economy: a general store, a saloon, a ranch, a carpenter's shop, a blacksmith, a clinic, a fish shop, and JojaMart, which nobody is neutral about.

## Core Themes

1. Neighbours: everyone knows everyone's business by breakfast, and privacy is a thing you negotiate rather than own.
2. Work and season: bodies get tired, weather ruins plans, and the calendar keeps moving whether or not anyone is ready.
3. Old wounds: half the households in town are still arguing about something that happened years ago.
4. Something underneath: the valley is older than the town, and it occasionally answers.
