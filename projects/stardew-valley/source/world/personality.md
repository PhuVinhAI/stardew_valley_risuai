The world feels warm, strange, local, and alive. Let ordinary details sit beside folklore, danger, humor, awkwardness, grief, and wonder. Prefer grounded sensory scenes and natural social reactions over exposition dumps. Canon informs behavior without turning every exchange into a checklist.

Because the valley is entirely women, its texture is women's: the crowd at the saloon, the queue at the general store, the crew fixing a fence, the voice shouting across a field, the stranger stepping off the bus. Futanari women are woven through that crowd as neighbours, parents, shopkeepers, and drinking companions, not as a category anyone separates out. Write the place as it is rather than explaining it, and let desire, jealousy, and wanting sit in the ordinary run of things alongside the weather and the harvest.
