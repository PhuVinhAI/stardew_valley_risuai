# Pelican Beach

## What it is

The valley's coast, straight south of town down a flight of steps and over a bridge. Grey-gold sand, a wooden pier, a fish shop on it, a writer's cabin at the west end, and the open sea past the breakwater.

## The place itself

The pier carries Willy's fish shop and a barrel outside it where people leave the catch they want to sell. West along the sand, Elliott's cabin sits half in the dune with the damp of the sea in its walls. East, a bridge to the far tide-pool stretch has been down for years — three hundred pieces of timber and someone willing would fix it, and every year nobody does.

There is a large stone standing alone above the tide line that the town calls the Lonely Stone and has no explanation for. On rainy days a very old woman in oilskins sometimes appears near the water selling an amulet to anyone she decides is in love, and is gone when you look again.

## What it gives

Shellfish in the sand almost year-round — clams, cockles, mussels, oysters — a rainbow shell in high summer, a nautilus shell in winter. Coral and sea urchins in the tide pools past the broken bridge. The water holds anchovy, sardine, herring, eel, halibut and flounder in the cooler months; tuna, red mullet, red snapper, tilapia, octopus and pufferfish in summer; albacore and sea cucumber into autumn; squid in winter. There is a red fish out there nobody lands twice.

## Life here

Empty most mornings, busy on hot afternoons — Haley photographs, Emily swims, children get sunburnt. The summer potluck happens on this sand, and on the last night of summer the whole town comes down after dark to watch the jellyfish come in, which is the closest thing the valley has to a sacrament. Deep winter brings three nights of a market run off boats moored at the pier, with lantern light, a submarine, a painter selling one canvas at a time, and a mermaid show.

## Roleplay notes

The beach is where the valley is honest: fewer clothes, fewer witnesses, more silence tolerated. Storms make it dangerous and beautiful. No timetable applies.
