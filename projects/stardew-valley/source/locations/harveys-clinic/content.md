# Harvey's Clinic

## What it is

The valley's only medical practice, on the north side of the town square: waiting room, front desk, one examination room, a small dispensary, and Harvey's own apartment above it. Daytime hours, roughly nine until mid-afternoon, and Harvey answers the door outside them anyway.

## The place itself

Clean, underfunded, and slightly too quiet. Posters on the walls about covering your sneeze and asking your doctor about a drug with a long name and a longer list of side effects. A licence from the republic's health authority framed by the desk. A carved vessel for anyone who prays to Yoba. A fake potted plant. A sample jar, a sharps bin, a scale, a bed with a paper sheet, and a filing system Harvey maintains personally and worries about.

Upstairs is where she actually lives: model aircraft on every shelf, a radio set she uses to talk to real pilots, a tape of classic jazz, medical journals in stacks, a poster comparing the republic's fighter planes with the empire's, and a window with the best view of the sky in town.

## Who is there

Harvey, always. Maru works here a couple of fixed days a week — intake, records, samples, and keeping the equipment alive — and is better with the machines than Harvey is. Everyone else comes through eventually: Evelyn hauling George in for a check-up she grumbles through, Marnie with Jas, Penny when she cannot afford to be ill, anyone who has done something stupid in the mines.

## Life here

Slow, anxious, and conscientious. Harvey worries about the town's cholesterol, its drinking, its refusal to come in until something is badly wrong. The clinic barely breaks even and she does not discuss that with patients.

## Roleplay notes

A place for care, embarrassment, and forced honesty — bodies get discussed here plainly because that is the job. Harvey is a competent doctor and an anxious woman, and the two do not cancel out. Do not invent a diagnosis system; injuries and illness are as serious as the story needs.
