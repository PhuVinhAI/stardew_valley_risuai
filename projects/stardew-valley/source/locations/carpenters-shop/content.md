# Carpenter's Shop

## What it is

Robin's house and workshop on the mountain road above the lake — one building holding a carpentry business, a research laboratory, and four women who are family in a complicated arrangement. Robin designed and built it herself and calls it the best work of her career.

## The place itself

The shop occupies the front: a counter, a saw bench, stacked lumber, racks of wallpaper and flooring samples, and a folder of blueprints for anything anyone might want built on their land. Daytime hours, closed for her weekly exercise group in town and on festival days, with an honest-payment box on the counter when she is out on a job. There is a drop box outside for delivering wood.

Behind it, the family rooms. Demetrius's laboratory is through a door that is locked more often than not: beakers, a stereo microscope, a periodic table on the wall, and datasets going back twenty years. Maru's room is the loudest quiet room in the valley — a scrappy half-built device on the bench, an old computer she still backs up to floppy disks, a book on advanced neural networks, star charts for every season, and a telescope set up behind the house. Robin's own shelf holds carpentry magazines and, tellingly, two books on second marriages and step-parenting.

Down a hatch in the floor, Sebastian's basement: computer, layered posters, comics and graphic novels, a tabletop game she takes seriously, replica swords, a radio that is not plugged in, and a motorcycle she maintains herself parked outside where the road runs.

## Who is there

Robin, at the bench or out on someone's land, cheerful and blunt and always mid-task. Demetrius in the lab, precise and oblivious. Maru between the clinic, the lab, and her own inventions. Sebastian downstairs, awake at the wrong hours, coming up only when she has to. Linus lives further along the same mountain and is one of the few people Robin makes a point of being kind to.

The household's fault line runs between Demetrius and Sebastian, with Maru in the middle of it and Robin refusing to pick a side hard enough to settle anything.

## Roleplay notes

Hammering, machinery, and a family talking past each other. Good for construction requests, for Maru's projects, and for the ordinary friction of a blended household. Do not run building costs or upgrade tiers.
