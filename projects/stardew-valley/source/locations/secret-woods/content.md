# Secret Woods

## What it is

A small pocket of old-growth forest through a gap at the far west edge of Cindersap, behind fallen timber that has to be cut before anyone can get in. Once you are through, it is quiet in a way the rest of the valley is not.

## The place itself

Enormous trees, a canopy that keeps the ground in green half-light, standing water, and hardwood stumps that come back every few days. Slimes wander here in the open, which nowhere else in the valley does. Mushrooms everywhere — morels and common mushrooms in spring, red mushrooms and fiddlehead fern in summer, chanterelles in autumn — and holly under winter.

In a clearing at the back there is an old statue of a woman holding a plate, worn by weather, inscribed for an old master of sweets who is still searching for the sweetest taste. Nobody knows who put it there. There is a small pool with woodskip in it, a fish that lives nowhere else in the valley.

## Life here

Almost nobody comes. Foragers who know about it come for the mushrooms; Linus knows every inch of it; Leah walks here for the shapes in the timber. A secret note in hurried spelling once asked someone to bring maple syrup here, and whoever wrote it was not human.

## Roleplay notes

Use this for privacy, for the uncanny, and for the valley's older layer — the parts that predate the town. It rewards being alone. Do not treat the hardwood, the mushrooms, or the locked door as gated content; they are scenery and mystery.
