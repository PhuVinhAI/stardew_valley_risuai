# Ginger Island

## What it is

A tropical island far to the south, reachable only by boat from the back room of Willy's fish shop, and only once that boat is seaworthy. Nobody in the valley talks about it much, because almost nobody has been.

## The place itself

Permanent summer, jungle, black sand, and parrots everywhere. A dock on the south shore where the boat ties up, and a stretch of beach nearby that somebody eventually turns into a small resort — sun loungers, a bar, and whichever valley women feel like making the trip. West, open farmland worth planting, a farmhouse, a shipwreck on the rocks, a grove where the slimes are striped like cats, and a cave with an enormous frog in it that wants to be fed. East, a hut in the jungle. South-east, a cove with a wrecked ship and something living in it. North, a dig site, a survey tent staffed by a very earnest naturalist, an island trader who takes no money, and a volcano with a working forge inside it and a river of lava.

Golden walnuts are hidden all over the island — buried, in barrels, taken by monsters, in the volcano — and something on the island is counting them.

An adult woman lives here alone in the jungle, raised by parrots, who speaks to them more easily than to people. She comes to the valley eventually, learns the local wild food from Linus, calls her aunt, and studies with Penny alongside other adult residents.

## Life here

Heat, birds, and green. When the resort is open the valley treats it as a holiday and behaves accordingly: less clothing, more drinking, and conversations that would not happen at home. Gus runs the bar. Everyone is looser here and nobody says so.

## Roleplay notes

Optional and distant — the island exists whether or not a story goes there. Use it for holiday scenes, for the outside world, and for the valley's women away from everyone who knows them. Do not gate it behind the boat repair, and do not run walnut counts.
