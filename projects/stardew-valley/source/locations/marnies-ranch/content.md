# Marnie's Ranch

## What it is

A working livestock ranch on the eastern side of Cindersap Forest, and the only place in the valley to buy animals, feed, and animal medicine. Marnie runs it, lives in it, and raises two of her nieces here.

## The place itself

Farmhouse and shop under one roof, barns and pens behind. The shop side is a counter, a bell, sacks of hay, shears and a milk pail on hooks, and a hand-lettered price list. Daytime hours, roughly nine to four, closed outright on some days; when she is out with the animals there is an honesty box on the counter and a note asking you to leave the money.

Inside the house: a kitchen that smells of something cooking, a stuffed bear big enough to fall over, a jack-in-the-box lying at an angle that unsettles anyone who looks at it too long, a coloring book, and a games console someone has been cheating on. Jas's room is small and tidy. Shane's rented room is a television, a microwave, frozen pizza cartons, beer bottles, and a radio used as an alarm clock. Marnie's own drawer holds long dresses and a pair of brown suspenders.

Behind the barns, tucked out of sight, is a coop Shane built for a flock of unusual blue hens she raised in secret and talks to by name.

## Who is there

Marnie, in the shop or out with the stock, who knows every cow and hen as an individual and keeps notes on their care she is happy to share. Jas, an adult and quiet, in and out of the pens. Shane, who works here without pretending to enjoy the shop part and comes alive around the chickens. Lewis, sometimes, and never in daylight — the two of them have a private arrangement the town half-suspects and gossips about anyway.

Once, memorably, the mayor's shorts were found somewhere in this house, and neither of them will say another word about it.

## Life here

Animal noise from before dawn. Feed dust and straw. Marnie walks up to the saloon most evenings because animals are good company but not enough of it. Anyone who needs an animal, an opinion about an animal, or a cat or dog rehomed comes to this counter.

## Roleplay notes

Warm, cluttered, and thick with the household's unspoken things: Shane's drinking, Marnie's worry, Jas's watchfulness, and a relationship being conducted in secret upstairs. Do not price animals or track livestock.
