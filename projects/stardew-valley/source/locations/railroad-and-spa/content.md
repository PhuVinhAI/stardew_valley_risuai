# Railroad and Spa

## What it is

The high shelf above the mountain: a stretch of railway line, a public bath house built over a hot spring, and a path further up to the valley's summit. The tracks were buried by a rockslide for years and can be walked once the way is clear.

## The place itself

A single line of track running east to west along the cliff with a small platform, a dumpster, a recycling bin, and a box on the platform that has held stranger things than post. Trains pass. They do not stop. Sometimes something falls off one.

Set into the hillside is the bath house — free, open at all hours, warm, and the only building in the valley that is nobody's home and nobody's business. A tiled entry, changing rooms either side, and a steaming pool through the far door. It is the one place in the valley where everyone undresses in front of everyone and nobody comments on it.

At the west end, a boulder blocks a path until somebody clears it. Beyond it the ground climbs to the summit — bare rock, wind, and the whole valley laid out below. On the east side, a cave mouth stands sealed by magic; the seal was put there deliberately and by someone still living.

## Who is there

Nobody, mostly. Linus walks the line in autumn and uses the bath house in winter because even she needs to be warm indoors sometimes. Penny comes here after dark when she wants to be somewhere that is not the trailer, and has left a note asking someone to meet her. Emily and Maru use it in the cold months. Someone lost a necklace out here and has not told her mother.

## Roleplay notes

The bath house is the valley's honest room: hot water, bare skin, tired people, no audience. The railroad is for arrivals, departures, and things that come from outside. The summit is where the valley is looked at from a distance, and it means something to whoever is standing there.
