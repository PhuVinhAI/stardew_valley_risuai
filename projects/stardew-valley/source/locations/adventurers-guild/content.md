# Adventurer's Guild

## What it is

A single stone-and-timber room beside the old mine entrance in the mountains, open from mid-afternoon until the small hours. Marlon runs it; Gil is asleep in the chair by the fire and has been for years.

## The place itself

A counter with weapons racked behind it — swords, daggers, clubs, a few pieces too heavy for anyone here — plus boots, rings, and ammunition. On the wall, a monster eradication list under a header asking for help keeping the valley safe: slimes, bats, skeletons, cave insects, duggies, dust sprites, rock crabs, mummies, void spirits, serpents, and a note that Gil pays out the rewards, which she does about as promptly as you would expect from someone asleep. Trophies, cages, and something moving in one of them. A back room Marlon does not open for everyone, behind a door that only unlocks for someone who has killed a great many things.

## Who is there

Marlon behind the counter: terse, watchful, generous with warnings and stingy with words. Gil, ancient and unconscious, who wakes up occasionally to say something and then does not. Clint sells and buys ore in town but has been in here about monsters more than once and tells it bigger afterwards. Anyone who has been in the mines and wants a better blade.

## What it does

Sells and appraises equipment. Buys monster loot. Gives a first weapon to a beginner who is clearly going down there anyway, because she will need it. Membership is earned rather than bought, and the lost-property service is a matter of calling ahead and paying the fee.

## Roleplay notes

The valley's one institution that deals in danger honestly. Good for mentorship, hard practical advice, and the sense that people have died doing this. Do not run a bounty tracker or a shop inventory. Marlon's missing eye stays a joke she refuses to explain.
