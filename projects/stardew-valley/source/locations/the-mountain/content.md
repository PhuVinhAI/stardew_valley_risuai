# The Mountain

## What it is

The high ground north of Pelican Town: pine forest, a cold lake, a working mine, a quarry, and the two buildings that sit up here because their business needs to. Three separate paths climb out of the north edge of town and all of them are a proper climb.

## The place itself

The lake is the centre of it — deep, cold, ringed with rock, with a wooden platform out over the water that is the best fishing spot in the valley. The carpenter's house and shop stand on the road above the lake's west side. East and up, the old mine entrance is cut into the rock, with the adventurer's guild in the building beside it and a minecart track running down toward town. Higher again and east, the quarry: bare stone, standing water, and ore near the surface.

On the slope above the lake, near the water, Linus keeps a tent, a firepit and a life she chose. It is not a hardship post and she does not treat it as one.

A section of the lakeside was buried by a landslide once, from corporate drilling nobody consented to; the company cleared it, called the operation entirely legal, and apologised for the inconvenience.

## What it gives

Leeks and horseradish in spring, spice berries and wild grapes in summer, mushrooms, wild plums and hazelnuts in autumn, holly and crystal fruit in winter. Coal and copper turn up in the loose ground. The lake holds largemouth bass, carp, bullhead, sturgeon in summer, and a legendary spring fish nobody has landed twice.

## Life here

Quiet, cold, and busy in a way the town is not: hammering from Robin's shop, someone hauling ore down the path, a light on in the guild after dark, smoke from a tent. Winter up here is serious.

## Roleplay notes

The mountain is where people go to work or to be left alone. Distances feel longer in bad weather, and the walk back down is when conversations actually happen.
