# Pierre's General Store

## What it is

The town's shop, on the west side of the square, and the ground floor of a family home. Seeds, saplings, fertiliser, groceries, cooking oil, flour, sugar, a rack of wallpaper and flooring samples, a bouquet under glass for anyone who has decided something, and a bulletin of local prices. Pierre buys produce and foraged goods over the counter at a margin she will cheerfully admit to.

## The place itself

Counter facing the door, shelves close together, a chill cabinet of fresh vegetables, and the town calendar on the wall where birthdays and festival dates are written up — the reason half the town comes in without buying anything. Nine in the morning to five in the afternoon, historically shut one day a week; if the warehouse store has come and gone, Pierre works seven days out of a mix of duty and dread. On the rare day she closes anyway there is a money box on the counter and a note in her handwriting.

Behind and above the shop is the family's home: kitchen, Abigail's room with the spirit board and the game console, Caroline's exercise cupboard, a shelf of books that includes one on surviving a difficult daughter and one titled *Why JojaCo Must Be Stopped*. Off the back is Caroline's sunroom, kept warm and green all year, with her tea crop in it. A guinea pig named David lives in a cage in Abigail's room.

## Who is there

Pierre behind the counter through the working day. Caroline in and out, more often in the sunroom or the vegetable patch. Abigail upstairs, or not at home at all. Half the town passes through in any given week, and Pierre hears everything first.

## Roleplay notes

This is the town's information exchange as much as its shop. A conversation across the counter reaches four other households by evening. Pierre's business anxiety, Caroline's separate green world at the back, and Abigail's need for a door she can shut all live in one building, which is most of the tension in that family. Prices, stock, and opening hours are soft — do not run a shop inventory.
