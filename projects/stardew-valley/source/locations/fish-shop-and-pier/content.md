# Fish Shop and Pier

## What it is

A narrow wooden shop at the end of the pier at the south of Pelican Town, run by Willy, who has fished her whole life and lives above the shop in a sleeping cubby up a ladder that is off limits to everyone.

## The place itself

Salt, tar, and pipe smoke. Rods on the wall, a tackle box open on the counter full of lures, bobbers, hooks and line, crab pots stacked in a corner, a bin of writhing worms that smells appalling, a chum bucket, a tank of small pink shrimp moving in circles, and something that looks like frog eggs. A model of a frigate on a shelf. Willy buys whatever anyone lands, at whatever the day's price is, and cannot offer discounts and apologises for it.

Outside on the boards there is a barrel where people leave the catch they want sold when the shop is shut, and a sign that says *Gone fishing, I'll be back tomorrow* on the days she is out.

Through the back is a room she keeps closed: her mother's boat, fifty years old, listing, half-rotten, and not seaworthy. She cannot repair it alone and has not given up on it. If it ever floats again she will take anyone who asks out through the south sea toward the islands she grew up in, and her shop hours change because she starts an hour earlier.

## Who is there

Willy, at the counter or on the boards with a line out. Kent, learning to fish because her hands need something to do. Elliott from the cabin along the sand, who talks to her more than most. Linus, who makes a better bait than anything on sale here and shares it with people she trusts. Anyone who has caught something they cannot identify.

Twice a year she hosts the valley's fishing gatherings — a summer trout weekend on the river and two nights of squid fishing in deep winter — and cuts her shop hours to almost nothing while they are on.

## Roleplay notes

Willy is competent, weathered, and generous with knowledge rather than money. The boat is her grief and her inheritance; treat it as unfinished, not as a quest. Do not price fish or run a catch table.
