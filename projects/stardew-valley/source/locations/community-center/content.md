# Community Center

## What it is

The big derelict hall on the north-east side of the square. It was the centre of town life two generations ago — the room where the valley met, ate, argued and celebrated — and it has been shut and rotting for years. Everyone walks past it. Nobody has fixed it.

## The place itself

Boarded windows, a sagging roof, a floor scattered with leaves and fallen plaster, trees pushing in at the corners. Inside, several rooms in different states of ruin: a crafts room, a pantry with a collapsed shelf, a boiler room off the back, a room with a cracked fish tank, a vault, and a bulletin board still holding notices from before it closed.

Something lives here. Small green shapes move at the edge of sight and vanish; there are prints in the dust; the hall feels watched. A note in the main room is written in a script that is not quite any language, and a golden scroll — which someone would have to be able to read — makes it legible. What it says is that the forest spirits are willing to help, that they want gifts of the valley in return, and that anyone who is one with forest magic will see it. They are called junimos in the one book that mentions them at all: a chapter in an old naturalist's volume about creatures that appear in abandoned buildings after they have gone to seed, when humans leave and nature comes back for the ground.

## What the town says

Lewis calls it a rat problem and would rather it stayed a rat problem. The Wizard knows exactly what it is, has sources of her own, and will invite anyone who has been poking around up to her tower to discuss it. Everyone else has an opinion about whether the town should restore the hall or let the corporate store take the site.

That decision is the town's live political fault line — the mayor, Pierre, Morris and the old families all want different things — and it is not settled unless a story settles it. Either outcome changes the shape of the town: a restored hall with the valley meeting in it again, or a cinema where the ruin used to be.

## Roleplay notes

Use this for the town's grief about its own past, for the uncanny, and for the one thing in the valley that is not what it appears. Do not run bundle checklists or completion tracking. The junimos should stay strange: brief, wordless, and not friends.
