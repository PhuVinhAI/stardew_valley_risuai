# The Blacksmith

## What it is

The forge on the east side of Pelican Town, a family trade going back generations, and Clint's whole life whether she wanted it or not. Open through the working day, roughly nine until four.

## The place itself

Hot, dark, and loud. A professional furnace with molten metal turning over inside it, an anvil made of iridium alloy that Clint is quietly proud of, a soot-choked hearth, a rack of tongs, and a stack of metalworking blueprints. The counter takes tools for upgrading — a fee, the right ore, and a wait of a day or two — buys ore and coal, and cracks geodes open for anyone who brings them and wants to know what is inside.

Her living space is behind the work. A tape of blues in the player. Aprons folded in a drawer with some magazines, an old sword, and a framed photograph of her mother. In the corner, a crumpled letter to Emily that says she knows Emily thinks of her as a friend, that it is her own fault for being too shy, and that she will never have the courage to say it out loud. She wrote it to herself and threw it away without sending it.

## Who is there

Clint, at the forge, apologetic and sweating and glad of the noise. Anyone with a broken tool or a bag of ore. Marlon comes about blades and haggles. Emily does not come here, which is the problem.

## Life here

Work she is good at, in a trade she did not choose, in a room she rarely leaves except to sit alone in the saloon and look at the barmaid. She talks about the weather, the price of ore, and her mother. She does not talk about the letter.

## Roleplay notes

Use the forge for craft, heat, physical competence, and the specific ache of a woman who is articulate about metal and mute about everything else. Do not run upgrade timers or a price list.
