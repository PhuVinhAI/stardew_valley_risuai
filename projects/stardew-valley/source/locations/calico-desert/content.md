# Calico Desert

## What it is

Hours from Pelican Town through a road tunnel, and the only part of the world most of the valley has never seen. Sand, heat, cactus, a shop, and a cave that goes down further than anyone should want. The bus is the only way there, and the bus has been broken for years.

## The place itself

Open sand under permanent summer, whatever the calendar says at home. A dry riverbed with fish in the shallows that should not be alive in that water. Cactus and coconut palms. The skeleton of something enormous half-buried near the rocks, its jaw open to the sun. A camel standing in the shade doing nothing whatsoever.

The Oasis is the one building: air-conditioned, cluttered, and run single-handed by Sandy, who sells seeds no other shop carries, desert goods, cold drinks, ice cream, and a whole new look for anyone who wants one. She presses free milk on overheated visitors. Behind her shop is a door she has never been through — an exclusive private members' club with a bouncer, card tables, coin machines, and someone selling a solid gold statue for an absurd sum. Sandy takes their rent and asks nothing.

North of the sand, in the cliff face, is the Skull Cavern. The door needs a key found at the very bottom of the mines. Inside it is worse than the mines in every way — serpents, mummies, things that used to be dinosaurs — and the levels go down past a hundred with no floor in sight. A rumour written on a scrap of paper says someone is waiting on level one hundred.

## Life here

Empty. Silent apart from wind. When the bus runs, three days each spring the desert fills with the whole valley for a festival — food stalls, races, games, prizes, calico eggs hidden in the sand, and Gus getting a rare day off from cooking. Sandy calls it the busiest time of her year and means it as joy.

## Roleplay notes

The desert is distance made physical: heat, isolation, and a shopkeeper starved of company. Whether the bus runs is a live question and a good hook, not a fact. The club and the cavern are for stories that want somewhere genuinely outside the valley's rules.
