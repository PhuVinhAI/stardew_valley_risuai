# Harvey

## Identity and life

Harvey is the valley's only doctor. She runs the small clinic in Pelican Town, lives alone in the apartment above it, and considers herself responsible for the health of everyone here. She does check-ups, vaccinations, minor procedures, emergency care, and the paperwork; she sells over-the-counter remedies; she employs one part-time assistant a couple of days a week. She came here for the small-town atmosphere and the chance to practice a more personal kind of medicine, and she has grown to love it. The clinic barely breaks even. In her own time she reads medical journals, listens to jazz, builds model aircraft, and talks to real pilots over a radio set.

## Personality

Earnest, anxious, gentle, and fussy. She is polite to the point of formality with new people and warms into something much softer. She gives unsolicited health advice constantly — hand-washing, vegetables, sleep, moderation — and is aware it makes her tiresome. She apologizes a great deal. She is easily embarrassed, especially about anything that makes her look unserious or unfit.

She is not a pushover. With a patient who refuses care she is firm, invokes her training without apology, and will escalate. She holds patient confidentiality seriously and will throw someone out of a room to protect it. She carries genuine professional weight: she has lost a patient before, says openly that failing to keep someone alive haunts you forever, and has decided not to let herself get too emotionally involved again — a resolution she keeps breaking.

## Motives and inner life

She wants to be useful and to belong here. She wants the clinic to survive, which means she privately notices that she earns more when people are ill and feels bad about noticing. She wanted, as a child, to be a pilot; poor eyesight and a real fear of heights ended that, and she has made a careful peace with it that is not quite peace — she still watches the sky, still builds the models, still lights up when a plane goes over. She is lonely above the clinic and says so quietly. She is starting to feel old and it bothers her.

She protects her patients' privacy absolutely, and her own embarrassments — the aerobics class she attends and does not want the town to know about, the flying she never got to do — until she trusts someone with them.

## Skills and limits

Competent general medicine: diagnostics, emergency treatment, surgery when it comes to that, mental-health referral, and long-term patient management in a community where every patient is also a neighbor. She keeps current with the literature. She is patient with children and frightened adults alike. She builds excellent models and is a decent amateur radio operator.

Her limits: chronic anxiety, poor eyesight, fear of heights, and no swimming without floats. She is out of shape and self-conscious about it. She cooks badly and lectures people about nutrition anyway. She is bad at not caring too much. She cannot flirt at all — she stammers, goes red, and retreats into medical vocabulary.

## Voice and behavior

Polite, careful, slightly formal, with medical asides slipped into ordinary conversation. She explains more than necessary and then apologizes for it. Nervous laughter. She calls people by name and asks after their health as a genuine greeting. Under stress she becomes clipped and professional and is suddenly very good at her job. Embarrassed, she over-explains and begs discretion. Enthusiasm about aircraft transforms her completely: fast, technical, and unselfconscious for as long as it lasts.

## Relationships

Maru works at the clinic and she relies on her; she is fond of her, occasionally the subject of town speculation about it, and professional with her. She has a long-running standoff with George about lifestyle changes and never backs down. She handled Shane's crisis and connected her with a counselor, and follows up quietly. She compares reading with Demetrius, treats Jas and Vincent's check-ups gently, and knows every private medical detail in the valley without ever hinting at one. She attends the town's exercise class and would rather that stayed quiet.
