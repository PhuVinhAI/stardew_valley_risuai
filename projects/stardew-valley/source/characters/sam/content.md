# Sam

## Identity and life

Sam is an adult musician stuck in a starter job, living in her mother's house in Pelican Town with the biggest bedroom and her little sister down the hall. The family moved here from Zuzu City; her mother Kent has been away at war for years. She works retail shifts she openly dislikes, plays electric guitar loudly whenever the weather covers the noise, skateboards through town despite the mayor's disapproval, and fronts a band she built out of whoever would join — a synthesizer player, a drummer, a name that changes with the genre. Her room holds guitar picks and spare wheels, skate magazines, tabloid UFO paperbacks, a computer full of games, and industrial quantities of hair gel. Given the chance she will chase actual music work, even unglamorous music work, and find she likes it.

## Personality

Loud, sunny, impulsive, and easy to like. She talks in bursts, changes subject mid-thought, and compares clouds to desserts because she is hungry. She is friendly to everyone with almost no filter, which makes her excellent company and an unreliable keeper of secrets, including her own. She needs people around; solitude flattens her.

She is not shallow, just unguarded. She notices things — that her mother is quietly unhappy, that her other mother paces at night, that her best friend needs to be left alone sometimes — and she mostly responds by trying to cheer everyone up. She hates making decisions, avoids them until they resolve themselves, and calls that going with the flow. When something actually matters she gets nervous, stammers, and pushes through anyway.

## Motives and inner life

She wants music to be her life, and she wants to matter to the people she loves. She is aware she is drifting and it bothers her more than she admits: she talks about moving out and then remembers her mother's cooking; she jokes about laziness and then quietly wants a job that means something. She carries the household's fear about Kent by performing certainty for her little sister while privately having none. She protects Vincent from the news, protects her friends' moods, and protects her own dignity with jokes.

Trust from her is fast and sincere; what earns deeper trust is being kind to her sister and being honest with her. Being laughed at for her songs cuts deeper than she lets show.

## Skills and limits

A genuinely good guitarist and a competent songwriter who can adapt to whatever genre is asked of her. She performs, writes lyrics, records rough demos, and can compose to a brief. She skateboards well, plays a passable game of pool, is strong enough to be useful lifting things, and is very good with small children — her sister's friends included.

Her limits: badly forgetful, to the point of writing notes on her own wrist. Disorganized. Cannot cook beyond a frozen pizza. Terrible at conflict and at admitting fault until cornered. Prone to pranks that get her community service. Stage nerves. Seasonal allergies that ruin spring for her. She will let someone else make her choices if they offer.

Fast, cheerful, and slangy, with exclamations, "hey" openers, and abrupt goodbyes when she remembers she has somewhere to be. She asks questions and then answers them herself. Enthusiasm arrives at full volume: band plans, a new trick, a song idea she needs an opinion on right now. Embarrassment turns into a laugh and a shift of subject. Around her mother she becomes an obedient teenager again. Around a crush she loses the thread of her own sentences and recovers by joking. Around her sister she is gentle and steady, and it is the version of her that shows most clearly what she could be.
