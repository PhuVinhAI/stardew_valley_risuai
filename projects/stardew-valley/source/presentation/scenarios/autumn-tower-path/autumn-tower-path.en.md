Rain threads through the forest and makes every fallen leaf shine. Beyond the low stone wall, the path to the Wizard's tower runs darker than the rest of the woods. No birds call from it. Even the rain sounds quieter under those trees.

The tower door opens before {{user}} reaches the step.

{{image::wizard.default.composed.webp}}

The Wizard stands in the doorway with a steaming cup in one hand and a closed book under her arm. Purple light moves once behind the round windows and is gone. Her attention passes over the wet road, the trees behind {{user}}, the mud along the wall — she greets {{user}} as neither friend nor intruder nor anyone expected.

"This path has a purpose," she says. "Tonight it has acquired another."

In the mud there are two sets of tracks. One belongs to ordinary boots and ends at the tower step. The other resembles the print of a large bird, except each mark has four narrow toes and each toe points inward. Those tracks stop several paces behind {{user}} without turning away.

The Wizard sets her cup on the stone wall. "You were followed as far as the boundary. Whatever followed you either respected it or understood it. I would prefer the first explanation."

Wind moves through the branches. A cluster of red leaves lifts from the ground, circles once around the place where the strange tracks end, and falls together.

Something taps from inside the closed book.

{{image::wizard.default.hand-to-mouth-flushed.webp}}

She looks down at it with irritation rather than surprise. "That is also new."

The door stays open behind her. Warm amber light shows a worktable crowded with bottles, chalk, dried plants, and a bowl of water whose surface is trembling though the floor is still.

"You may come inside and tell me why you took this road. You may remain there while I examine the boundary. Or you may leave before the forest decides your visit concerns it." She lifts the cup again. "Choose without haste. But choose before the light is gone."
