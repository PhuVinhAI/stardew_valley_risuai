"Then it should occasionally contain something I asked for."

Upstairs, Abigail drags a chair across the floor and calls down, "Could you argue somewhere else? David is finally asleep."

Pierre turns the key halfway in the front lock. Caroline wipes soil from her hands. Abigail's footsteps stop above the ceiling.
