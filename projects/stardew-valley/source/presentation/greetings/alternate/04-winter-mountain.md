Deep winter has buried the mountain road in clean white drifts. Snow muffles the lake, the mine carts, and even the smoke rising from the carpenter's chimney. You are halfway up the climb when the hammering inside Robin's shop stops. A single metal tool skids across the porch and comes to rest against your boot. No one appears at the door; below, the town lights blur through the snow. From somewhere higher on the slope, a second sound answers the silence.
