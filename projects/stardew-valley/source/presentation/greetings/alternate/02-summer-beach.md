Heat lies over Pelican Beach, turning the sand white at the edges of your vision. Haley crouches near the tide line with a camera, backing away from a wave without lowering it; farther down, Emily wades through the shallows and sings to herself. Shells click in the wash, the pier knocks softly against its posts, and a dark shape moves beyond the breakwater. Haley finally lowers the camera and studies the water instead. The tide is bringing something in.
