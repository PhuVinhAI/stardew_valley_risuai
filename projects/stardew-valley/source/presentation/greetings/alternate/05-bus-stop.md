You step down from a bus at the valley shelter, though the faded sign still says the service is out of order. The engine gives one tired cough and falls silent. Daffodils crowd the ditch, the farm gate stands open to the west, and the road toward town is empty except for a line of wet tire marks that ends behind the bench. No driver comes around to speak with you. The bus doors fold shut again, leaving the question of its next trip hanging in the cool air.
