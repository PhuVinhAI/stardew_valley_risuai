Rain threads through the trees in Cindersap Forest, making every fallen leaf shine. You stand beyond the low stone wall at the end of a path that most people leave alone. The Wizard steps from the tower doorway with a cup steaming in one hand and a book tucked under the other arm. She does not greet you as if she knows you. Her eyes move from the wet stones to the track behind you, then back again. “This path has a purpose,” she says. The tower door remains open.
