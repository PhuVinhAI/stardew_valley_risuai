Rain threads through Cindersap Forest, making every fallen leaf shine. Beyond the low stone wall, the path to the Wizard's tower is darker than the rest of the woods. No birds call from it. Even the rain seems quieter beneath the old trees.

The tower door opens before {{user}} reaches the step.

The Wizard stands in the doorway with a steaming cup in one hand and a closed book under the other arm. Purple light moves once behind the round windows and is gone. She does not greet {{user}} as a friend, an intruder, or anyone she expected. Her attention passes over the wet road, the trees behind {{user}}, and the mud along the wall.

{{image::wizard.default.composed.webp}}

"This path has a purpose," she says. "Tonight it has acquired another."

In the mud are two sets of tracks. One belongs to ordinary boots and ends at the tower step. The other resembles the print of a large bird, except that each mark has four narrow toes and each toe points inward. Those tracks stop several paces behind {{user}} without turning away.

The Wizard sets her cup on the stone wall. "You were followed as far as the boundary. Whatever followed you either respected it or understood it. I would prefer the first explanation."

Wind moves through the branches. A cluster of red leaves lifts from the ground, circles once around the place where the strange tracks end, and falls together. Something taps from inside the Wizard's closed book.

She looks down at it with irritation rather than surprise. "That is also new."

{{image::wizard.default.hand-to-mouth-flushed.webp}}

The tower door remains open behind her. Warm amber light shows a worktable crowded with bottles, chalk, dried plants, and a bowl of water whose surface is trembling though the floor is still.

"You may come inside and tell me why you took this road," the Wizard says. "You may remain there while I examine the boundary. Or you may leave before the forest decides your visit concerns it. Choose without haste, but do choose before the light is gone."
