Pierre is pulling the general store's ledger shut when Caroline comes through the back door carrying three pots of tea seedlings. “Those were not on the order,” Pierre says, tapping the cover. Caroline sets them beside the till and answers, “They were on my order.” Upstairs, Abigail drags a chair across the floor, then calls down, “Could you argue somewhere else? David is finally asleep.” Pierre turns the key halfway, Caroline wipes soil from her hands, and Abigail's footsteps stop above the ceiling. The front bell gives a small uncertain jingle as you reach the doorway.
