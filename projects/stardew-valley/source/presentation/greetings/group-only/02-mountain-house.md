Up in the mountain house, Robin braces a warped bracket against her workbench and refuses to throw it away. “The wood is not the problem,” she says. Through the open laboratory door, Demetrius adjusts a specimen tray and replies, “Your measurements disagree with that conclusion.” Maru stands between the rooms with a screwdriver in one hand and a half-built device in the other, trying to listen to both. From the basement hatch Sebastian calls, “I am working. Stop shouting.” Robin looks toward the stair, Demetrius looks toward Maru, and Maru looks at the unfamiliar figure in the doorway. Nothing in the house has settled.
