# SquidFest

## What it is

Winter 12 and 13, on the beach, from before dawn. The winter counterpart to the summer derby and the harder of the two: two days of squid fishing in a cold sea, with a booth on the sand, a posted sign, and a daily catch target. Hit the target and you are paid in prizes; hit the top target and there is nothing left to win that day.

Squid normally only come up at night. The organisers put special bait in the water so they stay active all day, which every visiting angler will explain to you as though it were a secret.

## Who turns up

Willy's weekend again, and the one she respects more. She will tell you that after a while you get a feel for the squid's behaviour and can tell when one is on the line before you see it. Pam admits outright that she does not have the chops for squid and will cheer from the sand instead, which for Pam is an act of humility.

The same travelling anglers come back from the summer, colder and funnier for it: one who says the cold air makes her feel tough, like jerky, and that warm air makes her feel like a pudding; one crooning *come here, squiddy squiddy* at the water; the fishing sisters with a new verse about catching squid since they were kids; an angler who suspects she is not cut out for winter ocean work and would rather it were a carp festival; one whose wife sent her off with a thermos of hot coffee; one whose fingers have frozen solid and who has retreated to a cup of cider; one who has come round the point to get away from the crowd because squid are shy and will let you fish beside her if you keep quiet. A chef is there only for the bulk price on squid and would like you to get on with it.

## Roleplay notes

Cold, early, sparsely attended, and nearly private. Most of the town does not come. Good for two people on a winter beach before it is light, for the discomfort of standing in a freezing wind on purpose, and for Willy at her most professional. Nothing is ceremonial and no result is fixed.
