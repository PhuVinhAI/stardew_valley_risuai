# Flower Dance

## What it is

Spring 24, in a clearing in Cindersap Forest west of town, morning until mid-afternoon. Late spring, everything in bloom, a stream running past, and the whole valley standing about in a meadow being awkward. Evelyn spends a week on the floral arrangements and asks everyone whether they like them. There is sweet wine, a red jelly that gets discussed at length, and a dandelion salad Gus makes on the spot.

The dance itself is a formal partnered set — two facing lines, a fixed sequence of steps, slow and stilted and a bit ridiculous. The older women adore it. Everyone under forty finds it excruciating and does it anyway.

## Partners, and the trouble with them

You have to ask someone. That is the whole social engine of the day: a public, standing request that everyone can see being made and being answered. Refusals happen. So do accepted invitations that surprise both women involved. Anyone without a partner stands at the edge, and there is no polite way to disguise it.

The mayor explains the rule to anyone new — find a partner if you want to be in it — and then waits to be told the ceremony can begin.

## Who is where

Haley has been flower queen five years running, is practising her steps in the grass, and has no intention of stepping off the throne; Emily eventually decides to compete for the title and has been working on choreography. Abigail hates dancing in front of everyone and comes because Caroline makes her. Penny is visibly terrified. Clint stands alone watching Emily and wondering whether she would say yes, then drowns it in jelly. Marnie says out loud that love is in the air and she is still single, and separately, quietly, that she thinks Lewis is going to ask her. Marlon, at the edge as usual, notices Marnie looks lovely today. Sebastian did not come by choice. Kent finds the open field unsettling and whispers herself calm. Harvey works up the courage all morning. Sam admits she genuinely likes flowers. Leah is glad you came. Caroline wishes Pierre would spend the day with the family instead of the till, and Pierre cannot bear to leave the register.

Evelyn used to come here earlier in life and says it has not changed. Demetrius traces the whole thing to ancient fertility rites and tells you that you ought to know about this. Linus brings jelly she made from the berries and offers it round. Pam is delighted: wine, warm air, a babbling brook, and did she mention wine.

## Roleplay notes

The most emotionally loaded festival of the year and the easiest to use. A dance invitation is a small public declaration, and the answer is real — accept, refuse, hesitate, ask someone else first. Nothing forces a partner and nobody is owed one. The clearing is also large, the trees are close, and people wander off. Who is flower queen is not fixed and neither is whether anyone bothers with the title that year.
