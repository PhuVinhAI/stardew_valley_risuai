# Spirit's Eve

## What it is

Fall 27, the town square, from ten at night. The mayor's letter says there is a chill in the air and it may be the approach of winter or it may be the tingle of a dark specter come to help the town celebrate, which is the most theatrical she gets all year. The square goes up in jack-o-lanterns, gravestones, and green-lit fog, and the town comes out in the dark to eat pumpkin food, drink pumpkin ale, and frighten itself.

Emily carves the pumpkins she is proud of; Gus does most of the town's lanterns and asks only to keep the seeds, which she sugars and eats. Caroline buys a pumpkin off whoever has one to carve with Abigail. There is a cauldron with something in it giving off vapour that makes people who stand near it lightheaded and reluctant to leave — Penny cannot make herself walk away from it and says her head is spinning; the fumes make other people unusually forward.

## The maze

The Wizard builds a haunted maze in secret every year and lets nobody near it while she works. She will tell you, if you find her, that the affairs of ordinary folk matter little to her but the elementals like a chance to see the town up close, and it was for them that she made the silly thing. Lewis knows only that the strange woman from the tower set it all up herself.

Inside are dead ends that were not dead ends a moment ago, walking skeletons nobody can explain, grasping hands, spiders, and — for anyone who gets all the way through — a golden pumpkin, which is valuable and useless. It gets larger every year. Linus goes in and comes out saying *good show, old friend*, because the two of them understand each other. Some people never find the middle. Abigail cannot go on: spiders. Haley is too scared to enter at all. Maru gets thoroughly lost and insists she has almost worked it out. Harvey hides in a corner because she got frightened and asks you not to tell anyone. Sam swears someone was walking ahead of her and then was not.

Outside the maze, Marlon has brought caged monsters and tells everyone to mind their distance. Clint says one of her iridium maces would make short work of the skeletons. Pierre runs a stall selling gravestones and lanterns and is quietly furious about missing the party.

## Who is where

Jodi will not let Vincent into the maze and Vincent sobs about it; some years Sam takes her in anyway and they get stuck. Jas begs Marnie and Marnie says no, and one year Shane talks her into it and takes Jas through very slowly. Pam is there for free food and will not go near the maze. Leah has had too much pumpkin ale and admits it. Shane wants to know why there is no more pumpkin ale. Robin thinks about stockpiling wood before winter. Evelyn wishes, as at the fair, that there were zucchini fritters. George eats the smoked turkey and concedes the festival is not all bad. Demetrius reports a study about music and cortisol and then asks whether there is ice cream for the cobbler. Elliott addresses the caged monsters in verse and says that sometimes one must stare into the abyss to stir a sluggish muse.

The shadow-dweller from the sewers comes up one year, is startled to be seen, and asks what you are doing here.

## Roleplay notes

Dark, costumed, crowded, and slightly drunk — the festival that gives everyone permission. Fear is a legitimate excuse to hold onto someone. The maze separates people from the crowd and puts them somewhere enclosed with no witnesses, which is the entire point of it. The vapour is a good device and needs no explanation. Nothing about the maze is fixed: it can be terrifying, silly, genuinely dangerous, or empty of anything but the Wizard's showmanship.
